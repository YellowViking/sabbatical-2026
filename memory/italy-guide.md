# Italy guide: mainland, north to south

Reference for a budget, solo, train-based trip through mainland Italy in autumn, built from research in Oct–Nov 2026. Sicily has its own guide: [sicily-guide.md](sicily-guide.md). Trieste: [trieste-local-food.md](trieste-local-food.md). What actually happened on the 2026 trip: [../trip-log.md](../trip-log.md).

Picks follow these preferences: outdoors and free first; no churches (church sights are marked as such); pay only for a sight that nothing later on the route does better (skip the Verona Arena for the Colosseum, Giardino Giusti for Boboli, Roman theatres for Pompeii).

Prices marked ✔ come from the place's own menu; "reported" means recent reviews. All prices are Oct–Nov 2026 unless stated.

---

## Italy-wide tips

### Route order and weather
- **Go north → south in autumn.** Italy cools about 1 °C a week and the south is 4–5 °C warmer than the north, so this keeps you at about 17–20 °C throughout.
- **November normals (Open-Meteo, 2016–2025):** Padua/Venice 19/11 °C, about 1 day in 3 wet; Bologna 19/11, 1 in 4; Cinque Terre 20/14, about 40% wet and 1 in 5 heavy; Florence 20/11, 30–40%; Rome 19/11, 46%; **Naples/Amalfi 19/12, 50–60% wet (the wettest region)**; Matera 16–17/8, 27–34%; Sicily 20/13–14, 31–40% (southeast Sicily the driest).
- So keep Naples/Amalfi short with a spare day you can swap, put Cinque Terre in the driest window you can, and give Sicily the last, longest block.
- **Lonely Planet sizing** (useful as a sanity check): Venice 2–3 days, Padua 1, Verona 1, Bologna 1, Florence 2 + Siena 1, Pisa a stop, Cinque Terre "over two days", Rome 3, Naples 2 + Pompeii 1. LP's Emilia-Romagna chapter says the region "warrants pumping the brakes" (Modena, Ferrara, Parma). LP calls Matera "unmissable" but "hard to reach".

### Trains
- **Trenitalia** (Frecciarossa FR, Intercity IC, regionals RV/RE): search and buy on lefrecce.it or the Trenitalia app. Regionals cost a third to half of the FR price for the same city pair and are often only 30–60 min slower. Check whether a regional is a **rail-replacement bus** (common on some lines, e.g. Catania–Syracuse and some Ligurian sections); the planner shows it.
- **Italo** (high speed only): quote its own euro price from italotreno.com; Google's train panel shows Italo in US$ about 0.89× higher.
- **Speed matters too:** if the fast train is within a few euros of the regional, take it.
- Sample fares seen Oct–Nov 2026: Padova → Venezia S. Lucia regional €5 (27 min); Padova → Verona RV €8.50 (1h33) or FR €16.90–18.90 (44 min); Padova → Bologna IC €15.50 or FR €19.90; Bologna → Florence FR €29–33 (37 min) or regional €10 (1h47); Florence ⇄ Siena RE €10.80 (1h28, direct); Florence → Pisa RV €9.80 (1 h); Pisa → La Spezia RE €8.90; Florence → La Spezia via Pisa €15.90–18.70 (about 2 h); La Spezia → Rome IC direct €28.90 (3h47) or FR €42.90 (3 h); Rome → Naples FR/IC €18.90–22.90 (1h10).
- Station left-luggage lockers (e.g. Stow Your Bags, €2.99 in Parma) make a city a lunch stop between two trains.

### Getting in from Slovenia
- FlixBus Ljubljana → Venezia Mestre about 4h25 (€30.99), then the regional to Padova (14 min, €4.05, every 15–30 min).
- FlixBus Ljubljana → Trieste about 1h35 (€22–24), then train Trieste → Padova about 2h20 (€13.90–18.10).
- Direct FlixBus Ljubljana → Verona about 5h45 (€21.49) or → Bologna about 6h20–7h20 (€28.99).
- The train route Ljubljana → Italy involves several changes and a walk across the border at Nova Gorica → Gorizia; the bus is simpler.

### Lodging
- **Compare checkout totals**, including the city tax (e.g. €4 per night in Palermo, Nov 2026) and checkout-only coupons. Agoda's checkout page can show a coupon the search card doesn't.
- **Price the hostel's own booking engine** (Cloudbeds, Mews, Octorate…) before any OTA; reach it from Google Maps / Google Hotels "Visit website". Ostello Bello, The Yard (Catania) and MEININGER were all cheaper direct in Nov 2026.
- **Ostello Bello** (Mews engine, add `&mewsVoucherCode=<code>`): `Weloveyou` gave −10% chain-wide (tested Florence, Naples, Palermo, Oct–Nov 2026); `NOTONLYBOLOGNA` −5%. `OSTELLOBELLO10` and `SOCIAL10` did nothing.
- **Party-hostel check:** Booking's "happy hour" filter is **not** a party flag. Instead, search each hostel's Booking reviews for *party / loud / noisy / music / drunk* vs *quiet / calm* (newest 25 matches per word, counting only complaints), and check the share of reviews below 7. Results from Oct 2026 are in each city section.
- Read the score distribution, not the average: cheap listings hide 1–4/10 reviews. Check for recent bed-bug reports.
- Google Maps' nightly seller price can omit fees; confirm the total on the booking site.

### Events that fill beds (check before fixing dates)
Booking's "NN% unavailable" banner for your dates against a week later exposes them.
- **Venice Marathon**, a Sunday in late October (25 Oct 2026): every Venice and Mestre hostel sold out, Venice 98% booked.
- **Auto e Moto d'Epoca** classic-car fair, Padua, late October (22–25 Oct 2026): Padua 85% and Bologna 91% booked.
- **Cibus Tec**, Parma, late October (27–30 Oct 2026): Booking showed 7 properties instead of about 255, cheapest €253; Hostelworld sold out.
- **Lucca Comics & Games**, 28 Oct – 1 Nov: 6-bed dorms in Lucca at €137/n; spills into Pisa and pushes up Florence (71–72% booked, about €95–101/n vs €73–80 after 1 Nov).
- **All Saints** (1 Nov): holiday-weekend pricing across Italy (Rome €111 → €80 after it).
- Also: Biennale Arte, Venice (open until 22 Nov in 2026); EICMA, Milan (early November); Florence Marathon (late November); Trieste was 85% booked in late October 2026.

### Sights that need booking
- **Vatican Museums:** slots release about 60 days ahead. **Colosseum + Forum + Palatine:** about 30 days ahead; morning slots go fast. **Uffizi / Accademia:** about a month ahead, named tickets.
- **Pompeii:** the timed-entry cap (20,000/day) applies in high season; from mid-October 2026 it was walk-in (booking still skips the gate line).
- **Florence's state museums are free on the first Sunday of the month** (long queues).

## Padua

A cheap, well-connected base for Venice (27 min by regional), Verona and Vicenza. Venice-island beds in late October cost €215–380/night; Padua avoids that.

### Where to stay
- **Hotello Padova – Hostel & More**, Corso del Popolo 2, about 300 m from the station: Booking 8.4 (914), Hostelworld 9.0. 10-bed and 6-bed mixed dorms, no private rooms. €28–35/night in Oct 2026 (Expedia and Hotels.com cheapest; Booking dearest). ## Venice

**From Padua:** regional trains about every 15–30 min, 27–30 min, €5. **No day-tripper access fee in October**.

### Sights
- **Rialto Bridge + market** (fish market Tue–Sat, so closed Sun–Mon). Go early, before the crowds.
- **San Marco:** the low corner of the piazza floods at high tide (about 95 cm and above); check the official tide forecast and go to Rialto first if a morning high tide is due. Palazzo Ducale €35 (optional; the free alternative is the Riva degli Schiavoni → Via Garibaldi waterfront walk). The Basilica *(church)* is €10 with a timed slot (tickets.basilicasanmarco.it); Sunday opening from 14:00.
- **Dorsoduro:** Accademia or Peggy Guggenheim (optional), the **Zattere** for sunset, Campo Santa Margherita in the evening, a traghetto across the Grand Canal.
- **Cannaregio:** the Ghetto, Strada Nova, the cicchetti bars on Fondamenta degli Ormesini / della Misericordia.
- **Murano and Burano** by vaporetto for an islands half-day.
- On marathon Sunday the Zattere and the Riva Sette Martiri finish area are blocked until mid-afternoon.

## Vicenza
Palladio's town, 15–25 min by train from Padua: Teatro Olimpico, Basilica Palladiana, a walk up to La Rotonda. LP gives it one day.

## Bologna

**Sights:** Piazza Maggiore, the **Quadrilatero** market streets (lunch), the Archiginnasio, the Asinelli tower (timed ticket), the Pinacoteca. **The San Luca portico walk:** 3.8 km under 666 arches up Colle della Guardia, free and outdoors, best late afternoon. Wine at **Osteria del Sole**. LP gives Bologna one day; it also works as a stopover between Padua and Florence with your bag in left luggage at Bologna Centrale.

**Day trips:** Ferrara (below), Modena, Parma, Ravenna's mosaics.

**Where to stay (Oct 2026):** **Dopa Hostel** 9.0 (2,270), central, about €45/night; **Bohoostel** 8.5 (1,985), 1.8 km out, about €27–38/night and cheapest on its own site; Hostel Il Nosadillo 8.3 (1,900), 600 m.

## Ferrara
Cycle the 9 km Renaissance wall loop (about 1h30 by bike). Bike hire: Todisco Brothers, 9–19. Easy day trip from Bologna.

## Parma
Works as a lunch stop between trains with a station locker (Stow Your Bags, €2.99). Beds vanish during Cibus Tec (late October).

## Florence

**Getting there:** FR from Bologna 37 min (about €29–33) or regional 1h47 (€10); from Venice FR about 2h13 (€38–41, direct).

### Sights
- **Free:** Ponte Vecchio, Oltrarno and Piazza Santo Spirito, Mercato Centrale, Piazza della Signoria, **Piazzale Michelangelo at sunset**.
- **Boboli Gardens** (unique; €10 on the day, €13 in advance; 8:15–17:30, last entry an hour before closing).
- **Uffizi** (the one unique paid museum): €25 on the day, €29 booked; Tue–Sun, from 8:15. Go at opening.
- **Accademia** (Michelangelo's David, €20 + €4 booking): unique but it's one statue; optional.
- **Fiesole:** bus 7 (€1.70, 25 min) for the hill views over Florence, then walk back down the old **Via Vecchia Fiesolana** (about 5 km, downhill).
- *Churches:* Duomo complex and dome climb, Baptistery, San Lorenzo, Santa Croce, Santa Maria Novella.
- Uffizi and Accademia are closed on Mondays, so Monday is a good day trip day.

## Siena (day trip from Florence)
RE direct about 1h28 each way, €10.80 (e.g. 08:10 → 09:38; back 17:18 → 18:52, Oct 2026 timetable). **Piazza del Campo** (free), **Torre del Mangia** (€10, 400 steps; a unique civic tower), the medieval lanes and the **Fortezza Medicea** ramparts (free). *Pici* pasta for lunch. Alternative: walk the Via Francigena from Monteriggioni to Siena (about 20.6 km).

## Pisa
A stop on the way from Florence to La Spezia (RV Florence → Pisa 1 h, €9.80; RE Pisa → La Spezia 1h15, €8.90): Piazza dei Miracoli and the Leaning Tower from outside are free. Hostel Pisa Tower and Safestay (€38–46/night, Oct 2026). San Rossore park bike hire runs only at weekends in October.

## Cinque Terre, Palmaria and Levanto

**Getting there:** to La Spezia, then the regional to Riomaggiore (8 min). From Florence via Pisa about 2 h (€16–19); La Spezia → Rome direct IC 3h47 (€28.90) or FR about 3 h (€42.90). Check for bus replacements on the La Spezia → Massa line; the IC is the fallback (about €11, 50 min).

**Trails (Oct 2026):**
- The coastal **Sentiero Azzurro** stretch Corniglia–Vernazza–Monterosso was **closed** (national park notice). Check the park's site before planning around it. The Cinque Terre card is about €7.50.
- **Via dell'Amore** (Riomaggiore–Manarola) reopened in 2024 but costs €20 for 900 m: skip it.
- **Palmaria island loop**: trail 510, 6–8 km; boat from Portovenere about €5. Portovenere is reachable by bus from La Spezia.
- **Levanto → Monterosso** high trail, 7–8 km, free and open.
- **Manarola** at sunset.
- **Ferries** switch to the winter service in early November.
- Liguria gets heavy storms in autumn; if an alert is out, swap the days with Florence.

**Where to stay (Oct 2026):** **Il Sassolino**, Riomaggiore, 9.1 (91 reviews), about €44/night, quiet. In La Spezia: **Costello** 9.6 (579), **Grand Hostel Manin** 9.2 (513, 200 m from the centre, near the station, €49–55/night). Ostello ninin de ma', Framura, 8.4. Private: Switch Holiday 8.9, about €51/night. Book early for the All Saints weekend.

## Rome

- **Unique paid sights:** Colosseum + Forum + Palatine (timed entry; book about 30 days ahead), Vatican Museums (about 60 days ahead; closed Sundays), Borghese Gallery (booking required).
- **Free / cheap:** Pantheon, Piazza Navona, Trevi, Trastevere, Monti in the evening; the **Appian Way** by bike or on foot (plus the catacombs); **Ostia Antica** by regional train. Rome works well in rain.
- **Where to stay (Oct 2026):** Lisa House 8.9 (male dorm), about €42/night, 3.8 km out; Beds&Rooms Tiburtina 8.9; Roma Scout Center 8.3; Free Hostels Roma 8.6, 2.2 km. Avoid **Generator** (school groups, noisy, 17% below 7).
- **Flights home:** Rome (FCO) and Milan have Air France / partner award routings to the US that Sicilian airports don't.

## Naples and the Amalfi Coast

- **Naples:** the Archaeological Museum (Pompeii's finds), Spaccanapoli, pizza, Napoli Sotterranea, the view from **Castel Sant'Elmo**. *Church:* Cappella Sansevero (*Veiled Christ*).
- **Pompeii** by Circumvesuviana (open daily); **Herculaneum** and **Vesuvius** are nearby.
- **Path of the Gods:** SITA bus to Bomerano (Agerola) → Nocelle → Positano. Save it for the driest day: Naples is the wettest stop in November. In late autumn only the SITA buses run on the coast, no ferries. Capri by ferry from Naples.
- **Where to stay (Oct 2026):** **La Controra** 8.9 (1,190), 6% below 7: 10 "quiet", 7 "calm" in reviews; Hostel Mancini 8.7 (2,266), next to Garibaldi station, about €25–28/night; Hostel of the Sun 8.6 (common area loud at weekends); Safestay Materdei 8.1, about €19/night; Ostello Bello Napoli (`Weloveyou` worked, Nov 2026).
- **Onward:** overnight ferry Naples → Palermo (GNV/Tirrenia, about 20:00–22:30 → 07:00, from about €38–43, Nov 2026); trains down the coast to Calabria (below). There's no direct Naples → Catania flight (ITA via Rome only).

## Matera

## Calabria (Naples → Sicily by land)

- **Cheapest route, regionals only (Nov 2026):** Naples → Paola (RE, about 3h20) → Scilla or Reggio Calabria, about €33; then Scilla → Villa San Giovanni → **Blu Jet** passenger ferry → Messina → train on to Catania, Taormina or Palermo. The whole Naples → Palermo trip this way was about €50, against €63.50 on the direct IC. Italo Naples → Villa San Giovanni €71.90; FlixBus Naples → Reggio about €25.
- Details of the crossing are in [sicily-guide.md](sicily-guide.md#crossing-from-the-mainland).
- **Stops:** Scilla, Tropea and Pizzo are the coastal towns on the line, but none of them, nor Messina or Villa San Giovanni, had a hostel (Nov 2026). The only one found was **Malalbergo, Reggio Calabria** (4-bed mixed ensuite, about €27, fridge only).
- Calabria was sunny in mid-November 2026 when Paestum and Maratea further north were rainy.
