## Sample plan — **3n default** *(slide hike day)*

| Day | Nature / sights | Food |
|---|---|---|
| **D0 PM** (arrive) | Bags · **Gamle Stavanger** white houses · harbour / street art · Breiavatnet | Light · **Kanelsnurren** · harbour **reker** if boats in |
| **D1** *or clearest day* | **Dawn Preikestolen** (shuttle) · summit lunch pack · PM legs-up lake walk | Early bakery · pack lunch · dinner **Fisketorget** soup *or* **Lervig Local** |
| **D2** | Easy city: Sverd i fjell (bus) *or* street-art loop · rain → **Petroleum Museum** · optional short Lysefjord cruise if skipped hike views | **Janas** smoked salmon · dinner **Fortou** / Pedersgata |
| **Exit** | Buffer for boat/bus/air to Bergen | Bakery + transit snack |

**2n compress:** Preikestolen on the only full day; city crammed into arrival evening.  
**4n expand:** hold an empty day for weather · or Fjøløy (car) · or Kjerag if you’re still hungry for cliff photos.

**Hike-day rules:** pack layers, water, lunch; start **before tour buses (~09:00)**; don’t hike cliff edge in high wind/zero visibility — take cruise or museum instead.

| | |
|---|---|

## Lodging *(hostels + budget)*

*Criteria: §7 · central for **Byterminalen** shuttle · free cancel while nights flex. Late-Aug ballpark · AI Mode Pro Aug 2026 — re-check Booking. **No curtain-pod hostel** found in sentrum (Pop-Up closed).*

### Where to stay
| Area | Why |
|---|---|
| **Sentrum / Vågen** | Walk to shuttle + food + Gamle |
| **Near Byterminalen** | Dawn Preikestolen bus without taxi |

**⚠ Avoid as hike base:** **Forus / Sandnes / Hinna** — cheap hotels, bad daily transit tax to shuttles.

### Picks

| Place | Area | Score | Dorm ~$ | Private ~$ | Bed privacy | Notes | Maps |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Central Stavanger Guesthouse** ⭐ | Brødregata / sentrum | ~9.0 | **40–50** | 75–110 | **No pod curtains** (open bunks) | Real central hostel vibe · kitchen · walk to Byterminalen | [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Central+Stavanger+Guesthouse) |
| **Central Guest House** *(Wessels gate)* | slightly W of core | ~8.8 | — | 120–180 | full private | **Different property** · self check-in flats · kitchen | [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Central+Guest+House+Wessels+gate+Stavanger) |
| **Home Hotel Skagen Brygge** ⭐ private hack | Vågen | ~8.5–8.8 | — | **150–230** *often incl. breakfast + fika + dinner* | full private | Half-board can beat “cheap room + restaurants” | [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Home+Hotel+Skagen+Brygge+Stavanger) |
| **Hotel Victoria** | Vågen | ~8.6–8.9 | — | 195–232 | full private | Historic upscale · easy shuttle walk | [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Hotel+Victoria+Stavanger) |

### How to choose
| You want | Book |
|---|---|
| Dorm / meet hikers / cook | **Central Stavanger Guesthouse** (Brødregata) |
| Private + kitchen, still central | Wessels **Central Guest House** *or* Guesthouse private |
| One-price food included | **Skagen Brygge** (run the math vs dorm + Fisketorget nights) |
| Zero friction dawn exit | Anything **≤10 min walk to Byterminalen** |
| Stretch 2↔4n for weather | Free-cancel · +1n if forecast is soup |

**Budget vs §6:** table used ~$180 all-in/day with guest-house lean. Dorm ~$40–50 keeps lodging under control; Preikestolen shuttle ~$49 is the big one-off (sightseeing/transport, not daily burn).
