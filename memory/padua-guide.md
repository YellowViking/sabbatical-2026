**My pick:** **Sun 25 at 21:00, evening visit.** It's cheaper (€10), gives you twice the time inside, and you'll be back from Venice by then. Backup: **Fri 23 at 09:00**, then walk 4 min to the station for a Venice train; that ticket also includes the Eremitani museums (Lonely Planet: €16). Shop: cappelladegliscrovegni.vivaticket.it. **Tickets are non-refundable** and slots are selling out.

**Urbs Picta card** (48 h €28, 72 h €35): covers the chapel, 7 more 14th-century fresco sites (Palazzo della Ragione, the Baptistery, Oratorio di San Giorgio…) and buses. **It pays off only if you add two or more fresco sites.** The chapel slot is booked inside the card's own calendar.

## 🗓 How to fit it in

**Thu 22 (today), from about 15:00:**
1. Drop your bag at Hotello. Try the Scrovegni desk for a same-day slot.
2. Caffè Pedrocchi for a mint coffee (11 min walk).
3. Piazza delle Erbe and the Sotto il Salone market (open until 19:30).
4. Palazzo Bo, outside only (tours are morning or 16:30).
5. Basilica di Sant'Antonio. If it's before 17:15, the Orto Botanico next door.
6. **Prato della Valle at sunset, about 18:15.**
7. Spritz and €1.50 cicchetti at All'Ombra della Piazza (until 20:30), or octopus at **La Folperia** (17:30–21:00).
8. **Hype Taproom** or **Bar Umbe**.

Walking directions: [Scrovegni](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&travelmode=walking&destination=Cappella+degli+Scrovegni+Padova) · [Pedrocchi](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&travelmode=walking&destination=Caffe+Pedrocchi+Padova) · [Piazza delle Erbe](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&travelmode=walking&destination=Piazza+delle+Erbe+Padova) · [Il Santo](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&travelmode=walking&destination=Basilica+di+Sant+Antonio+Padova) · [Prato della Valle](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&travelmode=walking&destination=Prato+della+Valle+Padova)

**Sun 25, morning before Venice:**
- Palazzo della Ragione (opens 9:00), or the Orto Botanico (10:00).
- Coffee at Steampower, open from 9:00 on Sundays (Lonely Planet: "probably northern Italy's best speciality coffee").
- Train to Venice about 12:30. Back for the **Scrovegni at 21:00**.
