## Lodging *(hostels + budget)*

*Criteria match itinerary §7: review **>8**, high count, **central**, social OK; typical cost not dump-tier. Late-Aug ballpark · ~10.5 NOK≈$1 · prefer **free cancellation** while nights flex. AI Mode Pro Aug 2026 — re-check Booking before pay.*

### Where to stay
| Area | Why |
|---|---|
| **Sentrum / near Oslo S** | Trains, Opera, Munch · airport train at door |
| **Grünerløkka** | Cafés, Akerselva, social · food nights easy |
| **Frogner / Majorstuen** | Quiet · doorstep **Vigeland** · Nationaltheatret metro |

**⚠ Avoid late night loiter:** plazas tight around **Oslo S / Vaterland** (gritty; fine transit by day).  
**Skip for this trip:** **Oslo Vandrerhjem / HI Haraldsheim** (Grefsen ~4 km, uphill tram) · **Anker Hostel** (*permanently closed*; Anker Hotel = normal 3★ only).

### Picks

| Place | Area | Score | Dorm ~$ | Private ~$ | Bed privacy | Notes | Maps |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bunks at Rode** ⭐ curtain | Grünerløkka (Rodeløkka) | 8.6–9.0 | **32–49** | 126–150 | **Yes — blackout pod curtains** | Best central hostel · kitchen, events, roof/cinema · light+power | [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Bunks+at+Rode+Oslo) |
| **K7 Hotel Oslo** | Sentrum (Kvadraturen) | 8.0–8.3 | **33–44** | 120–152 | **No curtains** (open metal bunks) | Poshtel · walk to Opera · kitchen · weaker top-bunk power | [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=K7+Hotel+Oslo) |
| **Citybox Oslo** ⭐ private | Sentrum by Oslo S | ~8.1 B · 4.2★ G | — | **85–116** | full private room | Self check-in · **no dorms** · guest kitchen | [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Citybox+Oslo) |
| **Cochs Pensjonat** | Frogner / Palace | 8.3–8.7 | — | **68–114** | full private room | Historic · kitchenettes · Vigeland side | [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Cochs+Pensjonat+Oslo) |

*Bed privacy: **Bunks official site** confirms blackout curtains on every bunk. K7 = bunks+lockers only, no curtain claim. Dump: `hostel-curtains-primary-vs-gemini.md`. Bring earplugs.*

### How to choose
| You want | Book |
|---|---|
| Social dorm + **curtains** | **Bunks at Rode** |
| Sentrum dorm, curtains less critical | K7 (open bunks) |
| Private + still cheap + kitchen | **Citybox** or **Cochs** (Cochs if Vigeland-heavy) |
| Bag drop / same-day train out | Citybox or K7 (Oslo S / Sentrum) |
| Stretch nights 2↔4 | Free-cancel rate; dorms sell out late Aug weekends |

**Budget vs itinerary day-rate:** §6 Oslo ~$185/day blended includes food+sights; **lodging alone** ~$35–50 dorm or ~$85–120 solid private keeps Nordics under control. Hotel-only centrals often $150+.
