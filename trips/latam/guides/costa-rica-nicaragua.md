## Open issues: Costa Rica + Nicaragua

1. **Chirripó beds not verified (needs you).** SINAC's public availability calendar (serviciosenlinea.sinac.go.cr → Disponibilidad → Parque Nacional Chirripó → February) needs a reCAPTCHA tick, and buying needs a SICORE account. I didn't tick it or create an account. Please check **Wed 10 Feb (preferred) and Tue 9 Feb** and book soon: 52 overnight permits/day, Feb is peak season. Then pay the Consorcio (Crestones bed + meals) when it emails you (an AI lead says within 5 days, else the permit lapses; unverified). The same SICORE account buys **Rincón de la Vieja** (online only) and **Río Celeste/Tenorio** tickets.
2. **Climb date vs arrival:** a 9 → 10 Feb climb needs the 16:00 check-in on arrival day (8 Feb), which only the 08:00 Tracopa from Paso Canoas makes. I recommend climbing 10 → 11 Feb and reaching Uvita the evening of 11 Feb. This also affects the Boquete section (8 Feb departure time).
3. **Nicaragua pre-registration:** sources conflict (State Dept: "recommended" for non-tourists; a SJdS hotel: required ≥ 7 days ahead for land arrivals; residents: rarely checked). It's free, so submit it by **13 Feb**. mint.gob.ni wasn't readable from here.
4. **Costa Rica exit tax ($8):** bancobcr.com showed a bot-check captcha, so the amount is from the Tico Times (Aug 2026). Pay online yourself before 20 Feb or at the border window.
5. **Cross-channel gaps:** Booking card prices for Costa Rican/Nicaraguan hostels may exclude 13 %/15 % IVA (not checked at checkout). Google Maps returned no seller prices for Feb 2027 dates, and Agoda wasn't compared for dorms. San Gerardo has only one dorm on Booking (Casa Colibri, $77/2 nights); Casa Mariposa needs an email for prices. Booking's ReviewList returned nothing for San Gerardo properties (no bad-% or location score there).
6. **Bus times still ≈:** Transmonteverde hasn't loaded Feb 2027 (times are from Nov 2026); the San Isidro → San Gerardo departures, La Fortuna → Tilarán → Cañas → Liberia, Liberia → Peñas Blancas and every Nicaraguan bus come from community/third-party pages (nicabuses.com blocked further requests after one page). Confirm at the terminals the day before.
7. **Volcano closures:** Telica is closed "until further notice" (Bigfoot, 24 Nov); Quetzaltrekkers León looks inactive. Re-check both in mid-February. Cerro Chato (Arenal) is still closed; Rincón's crater trail is closed.
8. **Uvita tides are an estimate** (≈ 12:15–13:55 lows on 11–13 Feb, neap tides). Check a real table a week before.
9. **Flight:** the Alaska award (25k + $94.42, AA1318 07:00) needs a Managua airport night on 2 Mar (≈ $35–60, not priced). 9 seats on 24 Nov: book soon. Cash fallback $417 (United 14:25 via IAH lets you leave León the same morning).
10. **Not priced:** Arenal 1968 (booking widget only; LP says from $25), Uvita waterfall fee, Somoto guides and lodging, the Rincón de la Vieja shuttle from Liberia, Laguna de Apoyo shuttle. Hotel prices at the Managua airport hotel.
11. **Safety:** Nicaragua is State Dept Level 3 (Reconsider travel, 14 May 2026): no drone, no photos of police/government buildings, phones may be searched. Costa Rica asks for proof of onward travel at Paso Canoas: the MGA → SEA award ticket is the obvious proof, but some officers want a ticket out of Costa Rica itself (a refundable Liberia → Managua bus ticket solves it).
