# Scandinavia: flexible options & off-beat ideas
*How to spend extra (or fewer) nights at each stop · nature-first*

Night counts are a baseline, not a contract. Keep the route order and move nights between stops. On a long Europe trip the Schengen 90/180 limit covers all of the Nordics too, so spend nights where they pay off.

---

## Where extra nights pay off

| Take from → | Give to | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| Gotland / Baltics | **Copenhagen +1–2** | Louisiana, Malmö/Lund, Dyrehaven, slower food |
| Gotland | **Gothenburg +1** | A full **Vrångö** archipelago day |
| Gotland / Baltics | **Stavanger +1** | Storm buffer / a second Lysefjord day |
| — | **Bergen/fjords +1** | A real Aurland overnight without rushing |
| Scandinavia trim | **Riga** | Cheapest base for day trips |

---

## Per stop: default vs stretch

| Stop | Default | Flex | Off-beat / stretch ideas |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **Copenhagen** | 3 | 3–5 | Refshaleøen sauna; with 4+ nights: Louisiana, Malmö/Lund, Dyrehaven |
| **Gothenburg** | 2 | 0–3 | Palmhuset filler; with 3 nights: Vrångö; 0 nights: go straight to Oslo |
| **Oslo** | 3 | 2–4 | Ekeberg, Hovedøya, Mathallen, Sognsvann; Emanuel Vigeland mausoleum **only opens on Sundays** |
| **Stavanger** | 3 | 2–4 | Dawn Preikestolen; Kjerag only after it and only if strong; Fjøløy with a car |
| **Bergen + fjords** | 6 | 4–7 | **Split** city + Aurland/Flåm base; Undredal; Rimstigen; Sandviken; in rain do Ægir, not a fog cruise. With a car: Hardanger, Sognefjellet, Gaularfjellet ([Bergen guide](bergen.md)) |
| **Stockholm** | 5 | 4–6 | Nacka / Grinda; keep Vasa + Skogskyrkogården; soft-pedal Uppsala/Sigtuna |
| **Gotland** | 2–3 | 0–3 | Visby walls + Folhammar, Gnisvärd, Fårö sunset; the easiest stop to drop |
| **Helsinki** | 2 | 1–3 | Löyly, Seurasaari, Lammassaari; Porvoo *or* Nuuksio with a 3rd night ([Helsinki guide](helsinki.md)) |

---

## Best flexible moves (enjoyment first)

1. **Copenhagen +1** after a long-haul flight if wiped out
2. **Split the Bergen block** between the city and a fjord base, even at 5–6 nights
3. **Gotland 0 or 2**, unless you want island days
4. **Gothenburg +1** only for the archipelago
5. **Stavanger ≥3** if the forecast looks wet
6. **Riga soaks up leftovers** before you burn buffer further south

### Pace-killers
| Idea | Why |
|---|---|
| **Trolltunga** | 8–12h day; duplicates the Preikestolen payoff; season usually ends early September |
| **Kjerag instead of Preikestolen** | Harder logistics; keep Pulpit Rock as the main hike |
| **Sigtuna + Uppsala + deep archipelago + full Gotland** | FOMO stack |
| **Adding another country hop** | Costs more than it gives |

## Between-stop freebies
| Leg | Tip |
|---|---|
| **Stockholm → Helsinki overnight ferry** | First 2–3h on deck at sunset |
| **Visby → Stockholm → Helsinki** | Ferry schedules thin out from late August: chain them or drop Gotland early |
| **Flåm in bad weather** | Museum + Ægir; don't buy a white-out cruise |
| **Bergensbanen Oslo → Bergen** | Sit on the left facing forward for the plateau views |
| **Harbour saunas** | Löyly (Helsinki) or Refshaleøen (Copenhagen) are easy upgrades |
