# Helsinki: short-stop guide
*A thin guide for a 1–3 night stop between Stockholm and Tallinn · nature-first · euro*
*All-legs transport index: [scandi-transit.md](scandi-transit.md) · Nordic money habits: [scandi-money-tips.md](scandi-money-tips.md) · Stretch ideas: [scandinavia-optional-offbeat.md](scandinavia-optional-offbeat.md)*

## How long

| Nights | Do |
|---|---|
| **1** | Suomenlinna *or* the Design District, then the ferry out. Rushed |
| **2** (default) | Day 1: city + Temppeliaukio + **Löyly**. Day 2: Suomenlinna or light nature, then ferry to Tallinn |
| **3** | Add **Nuuksio** *or* **Porvoo** as a full day (not both); keep Löyly |

| Idea | 2n | 3n |
|---|---|---|
| Design District / Kamppi | Yes | Yes |
| Temppeliaukio (rock church) | Yes | Yes |
| **Suomenlinna** | Yes, one block | Yes |
| **Löyly** sauna | **Yes** | Yes |
| Lammassaari / Seurasaari | Light alternative | Yes |
| **Nuuksio** | Usually no | Pick one |
| **Porvoo** | No | Pick one |

In early September the sea is cool enough for good Löyly contrast and there's still daylight for Suomenlinna.

## Getting around

| | |
|---|---|
| **Local** | **HSL** app: trams, metro, buses and the Suomenlinna ferry (zone ticket). A day ticket often pays on a Suomenlinna day |
| **Arrive** | Overnight Tallink Silja / Viking ferry from Stockholm: spend the first 2–3h on deck at sunset |
| **Port → bed** | Tram/walk toward Kamppi |
| **To Tallinn** | ~2h day ferry (Tallink, Viking, Eckerö; 8+ sailings a day). Tallink MyStar 10:30 was **€21.48** (Sep 2026) and lands at Tallinn's D-Terminal. Midweek is cheapest; avoid Friday PM / Saturday AM |
| **Onward flights** | Helsinki had nonstops to Prague (~€117, 2h10) and Ljubljana (~€120, 2h40) in Sep 2026, which Tallinn, Riga and Vilnius largely lack |

| Place | How |
|---|---|
| **Temppeliaukio** | Tram/walk to Töölö |
| **Suomenlinna** | HSL ferry from Kauppatori |
| **Löyly** | Tram/bus to Hernesaari; book a slot when busy |
| **Kallio** (food) | Tram north; cheaper plates |
| **Nuuksio** | Train + bus; a full half-day of logistics |

## Sample plan: 2 nights

| Day | Focus | Meals |
|---|---|---|
| D1 (morning arrival) | Drop bags · **Design District** · **Temppeliaukio** · waterfront · **Löyly** late | Market hall lunch · Kallio dinner |
| D2 | **Suomenlinna** morning · Seurasaari/Lammassaari if energy · ferry to Tallinn | Picnic at the fortress · ferry snacks |
| 3rd night | Full **Nuuksio** or **Porvoo** day | Supermarket trail lunch |

**Rain:** museums (Ateneum, Design Museum) + Kallio cafés; skip the open island in wind.

## Nature

| Place | Time | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Suomenlinna** | 3–5h | Fortress island: culture + shore walks. Ferry runs on the HSL ticket, so the island walk is free |
| **Seurasaari** | 2–3h | Open-air museum island, forest paths |
| **Lammassaari** | 2h | Boardwalks, birds |
| **Nuuksio** | full day | Real forest; worth an extra night |
| **Löyly** | 2h | Sauna + Baltic dip; the one splurge worth paying for |

**City:** Design District, Kamppi, Temppeliaukio. Don't try to "do all the museums" in two nights, and don't base yourself at the airport.

## Food (value lean)

| Do | Skip |
|---|---|
| **Market halls** for lunch | Esplanadi tourist terraces |
| **Kallio** casual places and bars for dinner | Hotel restaurants |
| Supermarket picnic on Suomenlinna | Mediocre harbour fish at harbour prices |
| Coffee + korvapuusti | Fine-dining FOMO on a 48h stop |

Finland is pricey: tap water, cook if you have a kitchen, limit bar rounds. Food gets cheaper in Tallinn, so don't blow the budget on two fancy dinners here.

## Beer & breweries

*Prices Sep 2026 · €1 ≈ $1.16. Restaurant draft 0.5L ~€8.00 (Numbeo), above Stockholm (€7.16) and ~60% over Riga. Treat it as a one-good-pint city.*

| Where | ~EUR |
|---|---|
| Neighbourhood pub pint (Expatistan, Apr 2026) | **€7** |
| Craft bar, 0.33–0.5L | €8–10 |
| Supermarket 0.5L domestic | €3.35 |
| **Alko**, Maku Cashmere NEIPA can | €3.99 |
| Alko, Mufloni DIPA · BrewDog Punk IPA | €4.29 |
| Alko, Thornbridge Hacksaw · Brew York Juiced IPA | €5.49 |
| Alko, specialty / limited releases | €7.63–11.99 |
| Barley & Bait, Tuesday special | €5 |

**Where to buy:** since **10 June 2024** Finnish grocery stores (S-Market, K-Market, Lidl) may sell fermented drinks **up to 8% ABV**, so nearly every IPA and stout is in the supermarket. Alko is only needed for spirits and >8% beer.

**Brewpubs to try:** **Bryggeri Helsinki** (central brewhouse/restaurant) and the **Suomenlinna brewery restaurant** by the island ferry pier. Neither publishes prices; expect the €8–10 craft band. Skip guided beer tours here: at €8–10 a pour they never pay off. Reload in Tallinn (€4–7 in taprooms, €1.71 a can at Rimi): [Tallinn guide](tallinn-local-food.md).
