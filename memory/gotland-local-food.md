## Sample plan — **3n if going** · **2n tight**

| Day | Focus | Meals |
|---|---|---|
| **D0 PM ferry** | Check-in · **ring wall** circuit · harbour dusk | Simple Visby dinner — not cruise-menu traps |
| **D1 island** | **Folhammar** *or* **Gnisvärd** · optional **Fårö** if 3n + wheels | Picnic (Willys/ICA before leaving town) |
| **D2 / exit** | Slow walls · bakery · ferry out | Station/ferry snacks |

**2n:** collapse D1 to half-day coast *or* walls-only + one short bus hop; exit evening/next AM.  
**Rain:** museums + wall walk + coffee — don’t force Fårö in fog.
