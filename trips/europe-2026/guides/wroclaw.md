# Wrocław: sights, food, beer, lodging & transport

*~4 zł ≈ $1. Prices checked Oct 2026.* A lively Old Town with a huge market square, bronze dwarfs everywhere, Ostrów Tumski's gas lamps lit by hand at dusk, and one of Poland's best craft-beer scenes. Cheaper than Prague for beds by about half.

## Food and beer

Prices are from each place's own menu (Konspira's website, Szynkarnia's PDF, Targowa's board, Stu Mostów's booking page); Ze Smakiem and Spiż prices from guest photos of their menus. Tap counts from ontap.pl, ratings from Google Maps. Distances are from Nowy Świat street, on the west side of the Old Town.

**What a good price is (Oct 2026):** pierogi **27–35 zł for 9** (the Rynek charges 45–56) · żurek **≤ 30 zł** · Silesian roulade **65–75 zł** · craft beer **18–23 zł per 0.5 l** (25+ is premium) · bread with lard **1 zł at Spiż** (27–30 elsewhere).
