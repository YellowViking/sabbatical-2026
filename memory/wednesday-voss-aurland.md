### Why the Snow Road, not the tunnel

**Stegastein → Lærdalsøyri is 40.1 km / 39 min either way — they're identical.** The scenic road is free, and Statens vegvesen's closure fields are **blank**, so it's open. Their own description:

> *"Combined with the Lærdal Tunnel, this makes a **unique circular trip** from Gamle Lærdalsøyri to Aurlandsvangen."*

**And the tunnel has no view either.** Worst case you drive through fog instead of rock, for the same 39 minutes.

⚠️ **At the 1,308 m summit: fog 85–97%, 1.5–1.8°C** at crossing time. You won't get the fjord finale. It's **above freezing** early afternoon — it only drops to 0.4°C by 20:00, so cross before dark, not after.

✅ **Stegastein itself has 0% fog all afternoon** — the platform sits below the cloud base. The view works.
