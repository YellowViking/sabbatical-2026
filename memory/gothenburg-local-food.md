### How to choose
| You want | Book |
|---|---|
| Social dorm + **curtains** / cook | **Slottsskogens** |
| Quiet dorm, curtains less critical | STF (open bunks) |
| Private value | **Hotell Heden** or Le Mat |
| Walk-off-train luxury-ish | **Hotel Royal** |
| Only 0–1n glue stop | **Inom Vallgraven** (Royal/Heden) — less tram tax |
| 3n + Vrångö day | Slottsskogens OK; early tram to boat links |

**Budget:** dorms here are a steal vs Oslo/CPH (~**$20–25**). Private value ~**$90–110**; Royal ~$150–190 if you want zero friction after CPH train.
