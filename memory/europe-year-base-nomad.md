## ⚠️ Constraint update — sabbatical year (no employment letter)

**Working assumption (you):** year-1 in Europe may be a **true sabbatical** — savings/runway, **not** a W-2 remote job and maybe **not** stable freelance invoices yet.

| What most “digital nomad visas” want | Sabbatical reality |
|---|---|
| Active **remote employment** or client contracts | Hard / missing |
| Ongoing **monthly income** above a floor (often €2.5–4.5k+) | May be **drawdown**, not salary |
| Proof you won’t work for a *local* employer | Easy |
| Private health insurance | Doable |
| Clean criminal record / housing plan | Doable |

### What that kills or weakens (typical — always re-read the statute)

| Path | Sabbatical fit |
|---|---|
| **HR / ES / PT / EE-style DNV** | Usually **poor** without qualifying remote income stream |
| **EE e-Residency** | Irrelevant to *living* there |
| **DE freelance visa** | Needs real freelance **business** case, not “I’m on break” |
| **Student / language long-stay** | **Maybe** if you enroll full-time and can fund it |
| **Financially independent / “means” visas** ⭐ | **Strong if savings (or passive income) are large** — see next section; *not* the same as DNV |
| **Schengen 90/180 loops** (“visa run”) | **Not a year-base plan** — risky, stressful, not reliable |
| **Georgia ~365d visa-free (US)** | **Strong** — no employer letter · low friction |
| **Albania ~1y visa-free (US)** | **Strong** — confirm current rule before relying |
| **Other non-Schengen** (e.g. Montenegro tourist limits vary; UK 6m tourist, not “settle + work”) | Case-by-case |
| **US home base + Europe in 90-day bites** | Still excellent · lower admin than any EU residence |

### Reframed goal for *your* year-1

Stop optimizing for “which DNV city has the best cowork.” With **high savings + no job**, optimize for:

1. **Legal stay without an employer letter** (means visa *or* visa-free *or* seasonal 90)  
2. **Doc type you actually have:** **lump-sum savings** vs **recurring passive income** (dividends/rent/pension) — countries split hard here  
3. **Work rules:** many means visas are **no work at all** (including remote) — fine on sabbatical, fatal if you later freelancing  
4. **Place for unstructured time** (nature, winter, social)  
5. Fall = **lifestyle scout**; EU residence = **consulate project** after you pick a country

## 💰 High savings — options that open (no job letter)

**Not legal advice.** Figures are **2026 research ballparks** from secondary sources; consulates change thresholds and *what counts* as income. Confirm on official sites + your consulate before applying.

### Critical split: **lump savings** vs **monthly passive**

| You can show… | Friendlier paths | Often hostile |
|---|---|---|
| **Big liquid savings** (brokerage/bank) | **Spain NLV**, **France visitor VLS-T**, **Greece FIP** (lump option), **Croatia** independent stay (1y), GE/AL visa-free | **Portugal D7**, **Italy elective** (want *recurring* passive) |
| **Recurring passive** (dividends, rent, pension, interest hitting the account monthly) | **Portugal D7**, Italy elective (if high enough), Spain/France/Greece too | Pure DNV still wants *work* income |
| **Only W-2 remote salary** | DNVs (HR/ES/PT D8/EE) | Means visas that ban work |
| **Nothing / thin runway** | 90-day tourist only · GE/AL if cheap burn | EU residence |

**Rule of thumb:** “I have a large nest egg” ≠ automatic D7. Many PT/IT files want **income that arrives every month**, not only a net-worth screenshot.

- A free pass to **live in Riga/Tallinn/Ljubljana 12 months** on tourist entry  
- **Schengen reset** by leaving for a weekend  
- **Croatia DNV** or **EE DNV** without remote income  
- Ignoring **private health insurance**, clean records, housing contracts, translations, apostilles
