# Slovenia: Ljubljana, Bled, Bohinj and the Julian Alps

Reference for a Slovenia stop. **Ljubljana** and **Bled / Bohinj (based in Lesce)** were checked on the ground in October 2026; the caves, Soča valley and Piran notes are pre-trip desk research (August 2026). Slovenia uses the euro.

**Suggested shape:** split the stay, about 2–3 nights Ljubljana + 2–4 nights by the lakes (Bled, Lesce or Bohinj), rather than all nights in the city. A Soča day from Ljubljana is too long without a car; it needs an Alps base. Related: [croatia-local-food.md](croatia-local-food.md) · [graz-local-food.md](graz-local-food.md) · [trieste-local-food.md](trieste-local-food.md).

**Rail replacement:** SŽ ran replacement buses on Lesce-Bled – Jesenice – Villach in autumn 2026; Ljubljana ↔ Lesce-Bled was unaffected. Check SŽ before heading north. An SŽ connecting bus links Lesce-Bled station with Bled Jezero station.

### Buses
- **Lesce ŽP** (beside Lesce-Bled station) → Bled: about 8 min, €1–2. Bled is also a 4 km walk (about 50 min).
- **Arriva Bled → Bohinj** (Ribčev Laz / Bohinj Jezero): about hourly, 32–40 min, about €3–4.
- **Vršič and Pokljuka buses stop at the end of September**, so the high larch hikes need a car in October. Rateče / Planica has a year-round bus from Jesenice.
- Ljubljana airport bus about €4 (skip the €35 taxi). Caves: bus to Postojna; train to Divača + park shuttle for Škocjan.

## Ljubljana

### Sights (free unless priced)
- Prešernov trg, the **Triple Bridge** and other Plečnik bridges, the Ljubljanica riverbanks, the **Central Market** (Plečnik's colonnade, mornings).
- **Ljubljana Castle**: walk up from Stari trg (grounds free; the tower view costs extra), then down through **Gornji trg → Stari trg** and the **Križanke** courtyard (Plečnik).
- **Tivoli Park → Rožnik hill**, an easy forest walk for sunset.
- **NUK**, Plečnik's National and University Library (check reading-room access at the door).
- **Plečnik House** (guided slots; book ahead) or the **National Gallery** for a rainy day.
- **Metelkova** art squat in the evening.

**Free walking tours** (yellow umbrella): the **Free Classic Tour, 15:00–17:00**, from in front of the pink Franciscan church on Prešernov trg (tip about €10 is customary); it covers the Market, the bridges, Prešeren Sq, Town Hall, the Robba Fountain, Congress Sq, the University and NUK's exterior, so do the castle and upper old town separately. The **Communist Tour, 15:00–17:30**, from the University building on Kongresni trg, **€15 cash** at the start.

**Two-day template:** dry day outdoors (castle and upper old town in the morning, classic tour at 15:00, Tivoli/Rožnik at sunset); wet day indoors (NUK, Plečnik House or the National Gallery, Communist Tour, Metelkova).

**Events:** the **Ljubljana Marathon** is in October (Sun 18 Oct in 2026, expo the three days before). Booking showed the city 92% booked for the eve, and the surrounding nights about €15/night dearer. Check the date before fixing a stay.

## Bled, Lesce and Bohinj

### Base: Lesce, not Bled
Bled fills up on autumn weekends (Booking showed it 84–86% full on two consecutive late-October 2026 weekends, and Bled Hostel sold out). **Lesce**, beside Lesce-Bled station and 8 min by bus from the lake, is cheaper and has the trains.

### Sights
- **Lake Bled loop**: 6 km, flat, about 1 h 45, free. Views of the island church and the castle.
- **Ojstrica / Mala Osojnica** viewpoint: the classic postcard view, 20–30 min steep climb (ropes, a metal staircase, roots, wet leaves in autumn). Best at **sunset**; bring a head-torch for the descent. Free.
- **Straža hill** for a short sunset or sunrise.
- **Kremšnita** (Bled cream cake) at Park Café (LP: the original recipe).
- Paid: Bled Castle (LP €17), pletna boat (LP €18 return) + island church (€12). Optional; the free viewpoints are better value.
- **Vintgar Gorge**: 1.6 km boardwalk, allow about 3 h. **€15 All-in-One ticket, online timed slot only**, entry 09:00–15:00 daily, **closes for winter after October**. The Vintgar e-shuttle from Bled bus station every 15 min is included; arrive 40 min early.
- **Radovljica** old town, 2 km from Lesce: beekeeping museum, gingerbread. Good for a morning before a train.
- **Lake Bohinj**: quieter and wilder than Bled. **Mostnica Gorge** (€4, open until 30 Nov) from Stara Fužina; **Savica waterfall** (78 m, €4, October 09:00–17:00, closes in bad weather; about 5 km / 50–60 min walk from Ukanc once the summer shuttle ends). **The Vogel cable car was closed for maintenance until 1 Dec 2026.**

**Two-day template:** day 1 arrive by train, lake loop, Ojstrica at sunset, kremšnita; day 2 Bohinj (Mostnica Gorge, plus Vogar viewpoint if you have the legs), back for Straža at sunset. A third morning fits Vintgar on a 09:00 slot or Radovljica.
