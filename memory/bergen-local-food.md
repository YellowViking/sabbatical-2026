**For this Sep 6–12 stay:** download the new **Skyss** app (journey planner + tickets), add a bank card, and buy the **315 NOK adult 7-day Zone A pass**. Start it just before boarding bus 12 from Bergen bus station on Sep 6; there is no tap on/off with an app ticket. Keep the phone charged and show the active ticket during inspection.

| Zone A product | Adult fare from May 1, 2026 | Use here |
|---|---:|---|
| Single · 60 minutes | 51 NOK | Only if making ≤6 total rides |
| 24 hours | 136 NOK | Good for a single transit-heavy day |
| **7 days** | **315 NOK** | **Best for Montana: breaks even at 7 singles** |

Zone A covers **bus 12, Bergen city buses, Bergen Light Rail, Fantoft/Troldhaugen approaches, airport light rail, Askøy/Nordhordland local boats, and local trains to Arna/Trengereid**. It does **not** cover FlixBus, Flybussen/airport express, the Voss train beyond Trengereid, or separately priced express boats.

**Fallback:** on a bus, buy a single or 24-hour ticket from the driver with cash or bank card; the paper slip is the ticket, and the same bank card can identify the purchase at inspection. This is not tap-in/tap-out. For Bergen Light Rail, buy before boarding using the app, ticket machine, webshop or SMS. Avoid SMS with a foreign number because some carriers block premium-message billing.

A four-day Bergen rental is roughly **US$194–220 before tolls, ferries, charging/fuel and replacement lodging**, and the car would spend its first three days chasing the same rain before returning for the Sep 12 friend road trip.

The only sensible optional pivot is **Thu Sep 10–Fri Sep 11**, decided on Wednesday night: rent in Bergen, drive a compact **Balestrand → Gaularfjellet** loop, and return Friday.
