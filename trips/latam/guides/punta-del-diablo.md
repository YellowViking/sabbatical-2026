## 🚌 Getting here: Porto Alegre → Chuí → Punta del Diablo, Day 19 → 20

**The night bus:** **Planalto 23:30 → Chuí 09:00** (convencional R$267.95 ≈ $45, executivo R$347.95 ✅ [ClickBus](https://www.clickbus.com.br/onibus/porto-alegre-rs/chui-rs?departureDate=2026-12-26), 26 Nov). Details in the [Porto Alegre guide](porto-alegre.md).

**The border (two separate stamps):**
- **No time change:** Brazil (Rio Grande do Sul) and Uruguay are both UTC−3 and neither uses daylight saving time (AhiVoy FAQ citing Brazil's Ministério de Minas e Energia, Nov 2026).
- **Chuí and Chuy are one town split by an avenue** (Av. Brasil / Av. Uruguay). You can walk across it freely; the passport posts are on the highways outside town.
- **The two controls are not integrated:** Uruguay's border page lists the Chuy–Chuí "Área de Control Integrado" as **"No integrado"** ✅ [gub.uy, Ministerio de Defensa Nacional](https://www.gub.uy/ministerio-defensa-nacional/chuy). So you need a Brazilian exit stamp and a Uruguayan entry stamp, from two offices.
- **1. 🇧🇷 Exit: Polícia Federal "Posto de Imigração Chuí", BR-471** ✅ (the PF's own Termo de Referência 17/2026, Oct 2026: "Posto Imigração Chuí – BR 471, km 650"). It sits at the Brazilian customs post on the highway into town, ≈ 2 km north of the centre ([on Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?ftid=0x950cdf0057627fb1:0xee1630669728438e)). The Planalto bus passes it just before town. It is a domestic line, so it doesn't stop unless you ask: **ask the driver to let you off at the Polícia Federal** (≈), take your pack, get the stamp and walk on into town. If the bus doesn't stop, take a taxi back from the rodoviária.
  - Optional: the PF's free online **pré-cadastro migratório** (QR code) works at Chuí ([gov.br](https://www.gov.br/pt-br/servicos/pre-cadastro-migratorio)).
- **2. 🇺🇾 Entry: Migración, on Ruta 9 just south of Chuy** ([on Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?ftid=0x9574bb342f4983b9:0xd5882c57bec83fbc); the official page puts the control area at Ruta 9 km 336; phone 4474 2072 per AhiVoy). **The southbound Uruguayan buses stop there**: the driver calls out "migración". **Get off and get the entry stamp.** A 2017 trip report on this exact route says the bus drives on if no one asks, and the missing entry cost a UYU 2,800 fine on the way out (Mochileiros, lead). Another report says the stop "takes over an hour" in total with the stamp (2015, lead).
- **Allow ≈ 30–60 min** for both stamps (≈, south-brazil check).

## 🌳 Things to do (free and outdoors first)

**Santa Teresa National Park** (free; open daily 08:00–20:00 from December to February ✅ [Servicio de Parques del Ejército](https://serviciodeparquesdelejercito.com.uy/horarios-visita-y-acceso.html)):
- **Getting there:** COT **14:05** from Punta del Diablo → park entrance (Ruta 9 km 302) **14:15**; back on COT **17:05 → 17:15**, Rutas del Sol **18:50 → 19:10** or COT 20:35 → 20:45 (winter times). Or walk north along Playa Grande: the park starts ≈ 5 km from town (Wikipedia).
- **Free entry** ✅ [Turismo Rocha](https://turismorocha.gub.uy/atractivos/aire-libre/parque-nacional-santa-teresa); only overnight campers pay.
- **Fortaleza de Santa Teresa:** **UYU 50**, **pesos only**; daily 10:00–18:00 from December to 1 March ✅ [Turismo Rocha](https://turismorocha.gub.uy/atractivos/historicos/fortaleza-de-santa-teresa). Its own gate is at km 306, or reach it from inside the park.
- **Invernáculo:** the park's greenhouse, built in **1939**, mostly tropical plants, with gardens around it ✅ Turismo Rocha.

**Or Laguna Negra** (free; flamingos, capybaras):
- Turismo Rocha: from the **Ruta 9 km 302 roundabout** at the park entrance, take the road on the opposite side and follow "a few km of winding dirt road" ✅ [Turismo Rocha](https://turismorocha.gub.uy/atractivos/lagunas/laguna-negra).
- The "mirador natural" viewpoint is **≈ 5 km from Ruta 9, ≈ 1 h 10 each way** on foot (OSM, ≈). No bus, and no taxi price found ("Taxi y Remise de Punta" +598 99 876 300).
- With the 14:15 arrival it's the park **or** the lagoon on Day 20, not both.

**In town** (tips from the Broke Backpacker, lead):
- 🚶 **The beach walk towards Brazil:** north-east along **Playa Grande** to Santa Teresa, often empty; sea lions, and penguins are possible.
- 🏄 **Playa Grande** for surfing or bodyboarding, ≈ 20 min on foot.
- 🌅 **The fishermen's point** (Playa de los Pescadores and the rocks of La Punta) and its sunset; the artisans' fair is on the point.
- 🏖️ **Playa del Rivero** (the surf beach next to the hostel) and **Playa de la Viuda** to the south.

## 🍽️ Eat and drink

- **Buñuelos de algas** (seaweed fritters) at the point: **Kiosco Alba**, a stand at the Playa de los Pescadores fair, from 12:00 (its Facebook page, lead). Prices seen: **20 for UYU 200** at the roundabout stalls (Instagram, early 2026, lead); 15 for UYU 120 in 2022 (Reddit, lead).
- **Empanadas Ta' Güenazo** on Av. de los Pescadores: fish empanadas and buñuelos, ≈ UYU 200–400 a person (Google, lead).
- **Fresh fish to cook** at the hostel: the fishermen's stalls by the point ("Negocios de venta de pescado fresco") and **Pescadería Punta del Diablo**.
- 🍺 **Craft beer:** the town has its own brewery, **Cervecería Punta del Diablo**, sold at refill points (Instagram, lead); **Tatayaba** (wood-fired oven, craft beer, live music; Instagram, lead) and **Maula Restobar** on the beach (craft beers, TripAdvisor, lead).
- **Groceries:** Supermercado Sandra 2 (with the BROU cash machine).

## ℹ️ Good to know

- **Money:** Uruguayan pesos; cards are widely taken, but the fort, the buses and the beach stalls want cash.
  - **ATMs in Punta del Diablo:** BROU, **24 h, at Super Sandra** (Av. General San Martín esq. Rivera) ✅ [BROU](https://brou.com.uy/en/sucursales-y-servicios/cajeros-accesibles); a REDBROU at **Paseo del Rivero** (Calle 9 between 12 and 14), **summer only** ✅ [Turismo Rocha](https://turismorocha.gub.uy/cajeros-automaticos).
  - **Chuy:** BROU on Arachanes between Gral. Artigas and Leonardo Olivera ✅ Turismo Rocha. Get pesos there if the bus timing allows.
  - **Santa Teresa park:** a summer-only ATM at the Capatacía ✅ Turismo Rocha.
  - **Cabo Polonio:** Turismo Rocha lists a summer-only ATM at the **Puerta del Polonio** gate (Ruta 10 km 264.5); there is none in the village.
- **Peak season:** late December is the start of Uruguay's summer high season; book the bed now.

## 🚌 Leaving: Punta del Diablo → Cabo Polonio, Day 21

There is **no direct bus**. Change at **Rocha** onto the Montevideo → Cabo Polonio line (winter 2026 timetable; the summer one isn't out yet):

1. 🚌 **Rutas del Sol 09:20 Punta del Diablo → Rocha 11:00** (the 07:30 from 18 de Julio / 08:00 from Chuy, daily). Later option: **Cynsa 11:30 → Rocha 13:00** (a 30-min change). COT's 12:15 reaches Rocha at 13:35, too late. ✅ [Rutas del Sol](https://www.rutasdelsol.com.uy/rutas-horarios) + DNT
2. 🚌 **Rutas del Sol 13:30 Rocha → La Paloma 14:00 → La Pedrera 14:15 → Cabo Polonio 14:30** (the 10:10 from Montevideo Tres Cruces, daily; DNT lists 14:45). **Get off at "Cabo Polonio", the Puerta del Polonio gate on Ruta 10 km 264.5**, which is also the truck terminal. It is the same bus as the Montevideo plan in the [Cabo Polonio guide](cabo-polonio.md), so the arrival is unchanged.
3. 🚚 **4x4 truck** into the village: ≈ 20 min, **UYU 470 return** ✅ [Turismo Rocha](https://turismorocha.gub.uy/en/schedules-cabo-polonio-uruguay); details in the [Cabo Polonio guide](cabo-polonio.md).

- **Fares:** not published for these sections ≈. From Montevideo, Rutas del Sol charges UYU 702 to Rocha and 1,003 to Cabo Polonio.
- **Via Castillos?** The winter Rutas del Sol Castillos line (Montevideo 15:15 → Castillos 19:20 → Aguas Dulces 19:30 → Barra de Valizas 19:50) shows **"NO"** for the Cabo Polonio stop, so it doesn't help. A taxi from Castillos to the gate is possible, but no price was found.
- ⚠️ **Recheck in mid-December:** the summer timetable may add a coastal run.
