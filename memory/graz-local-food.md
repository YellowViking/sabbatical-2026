# Graz: sights, food and beer

A good one-night stop on the Prague/Brno → Slovenia/Croatia rail line. Prices and hours were checked in October 2026 on each place's own site unless marked "lead".

**From the station:** Graz Hbf is 1.5–2 km from Hauptplatz. Tram 1, 4, 6 or 7 every 5 min (6 min, about €3 for an hour), or walk 20–25 min east along Annenstraße and over the Mur past Murinsel.

## Sights (free first)

| What | Cost · hours |
|---|---|
| Murinsel and the Mur riverfront | Free |
| Old-town lanes, Graz Cathedral + Mausoleum, the **double spiral staircase** in the Burg | Free; staircase daily 10–18 |
| **Schlossberg on foot via the rock stairs → Uhrturm** at sunset (LP's top view; open 24 h) | Free; the lift is about €3.50 |
| **Kaiser-Josef-Platz farmers' market** (from 06:00), then Stadtpark | Free |
| **Kunsthaus** (the blue "friendly alien" building) | about €12 · Tue–Sun 10–18 |
| Landeszeughaus (armoury), if you want one museum | about €12 · from 10:00 |
| **Schloss Eggenberg park** (UNESCO, peacocks), tram 1, 15 min | park about €3 · from 08:00 |

**Graz Card** covers the Kunsthaus, Landeszeughaus, Eggenberg and city transport; only worth it for 2+ museums.

**One-night template:** arrive early afternoon → Murinsel, old town, Kunsthaus → Schlossberg stairs for sunset → Backhendl dinner → HOPS. Next morning: farmers' market, Eggenberg or Mohrenwirt lunch, then the afternoon train.

## Food

Styrian specialities: **Backhendl** (breaded fried chicken), **Kürbiscremesuppe** (pumpkin soup), **Käferbohnen** (runner beans) salad and anything with **Kernöl** (pumpkin-seed oil).

| Place | Google rating | Order (price, Oct 2026) | Hours |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Der Steirer** | 4.6 (5,334) | **Backhendl** (½ chicken) **€17.50** · pumpkin soup €6.50 · Steirersalat with Käferbohnen + Kernöl €7.90 · Schweinsbraten €19 (from 18:00) · "Styrian tapas" small plates. **Weekday lunch 11:30–14:30: soup + main €14** (main alone €12.50) | Daily 11:00–24:00 (kitchen to 23:00) |
| **Wirtshaus Bernsteiner** (Gries, about 1 km) | 4.7 (222) | Cheapest Backhendl: **½ chicken €14.50**; pumpkin soup; weekday lunch menu | Mon–Fri 10:00–22:00, **kitchen closes 21:00** |
| **Glöckl Bräu** (brewpub by Glockenspielplatz) | 4.6 (5,808) | Käferbohnensalat about €8.80 · Schweinsbraten about €18 · beer €4.80 per 0.5 l | Daily |
| **Mohrenwirt** (Bib Gourmand) | 4.8 (1,090) | Organic Styrian cooking; book online, fixed time slots | Wed–Sat 11:30–23:00; closed Tue |

**Skip:** Altsteirische Schmankerlstub'n (4.2), Landhauskeller (4.4, pricey), Geniesserei am Markt (lunch only).
