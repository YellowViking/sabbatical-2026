## From Lonely Planet (*South America on a Shoestring*, 2019 research)

LP is older than the live checks above; where they differ, the ✅ checks win. Everything below is marked (LP).

**Paraguay:**
- **Borders:** no fees at any land border. Buses may not stop at immigration because locals don't need to, so ask the driver; without an entry stamp you can be fined when you leave (LP). LP still says Americans need a visa; that's out of date (✅ Ley 7314/2024, see the [Asunción guide](asuncion.md)).
- **Money:** ATMs in cities take Visa/Mastercard but charge ≈ PYG 25,000 a withdrawal, with a usual limit of ≈ PYG 1.5 million a day. Cards are rare outside the big cities and sometimes carry a surcharge. Keep small notes, and change leftover guaraníes before leaving, because they're hard to sell abroad (LP).
- **Safety:** one of the continent's safest countries; cities are fine at night except Ciudad del Este and Asunción's riverside Costanera. Expect the odd police bribe attempt or "gringo price" (LP).
- **Buses:** quality ranges from luxury to packed and stuffy; the better companies cost only a little more, so pay it. Drivers run the air-con cold (LP).
- **Daily life:** long siestas; many restaurants close on Mondays; banks open ≈ 08:00–13:00 Mon–Sat (LP). Tap water is fine in cities; dengue appears occasionally, malaria doesn't (LP).
- **Public holidays on our dates:** 25 Dec and 1 Jan (LP).

**Uruguay:**
- **Money:** ATMs with the Banred or Redbrou logo take foreign cards; US dollars come only in $100 multiples. Where hostels quote in dollars, paying in pesos often works out cheaper. No parallel exchange rate (LP).
- **Eating out:** most restaurants add a *cubierto* (bread cover charge); tip 10%. The cheap way to eat out is the weekday *menú ejecutivo*, US$10–15 in LP (LP).
- **Buses:** booking ahead is only needed around holidays, which is exactly these dates. On peak days a company may run several buses at the same hour and print a bus number on the ticket, so check it before boarding. Most terminals have left luggage (LP).
- **Taxis:** metered, +20% 22:00–06:00 and on Sundays and holidays (LP).
- **Coast prices:** the high season runs from Christmas to Carnaval/Easter, when beach-town prices jump and towns fill up (LP).
- **Public holidays on our dates:** 25 Dec, 1 Jan and 6 Jan (LP).

**Getting from Paraguay to Uruguay overland (LP):** the two countries share no border.
- **Direct:** EGA's Asunción–Montevideo bus (via Encarnación), ≈ 20 h, twice a week, Wed and Sat (LP), as booked here.
- **Via Argentina, river crossings:** Encarnación → Posadas (bus, train or ferry), then south through Argentina to one of three Río Uruguay bridges into Uruguay: Concordia → Salto (bus ≈ 1 h, or the Mon–Sat launch, 15 min), Colón → Paysandú, or Gualeguaychú → Fray Bentos (LP).
- **Via Asunción → Clorinda (Argentina):** the San Ignacio de Ayolas bridge at Puerto Falcón, with a local "Falcón" bus from Mercado Cuatro; LP advises against the Clorinda ferry (LP).
- **Via Buenos Aires:** Asunción → Buenos Aires by bus (18–21 h in LP), then the ferry to Colonia or Montevideo (LP).
