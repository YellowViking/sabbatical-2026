# Sogn car leg — Sep 8–12, 2026 · **NOT TAKEN**

> ## ⛔ Decided against, Sep 5, 2026
> **The car was researched, priced and then declined.** The killer was the transfer: **Bergen ↔ Sogndal is 237 km, ~4h11 by OSRM and realistically 4h30–5h with a ferry wait** — about **8h20 of the leg's ~12h wheel time** spent just getting there and back. The friend's Sep 12–20 trip already claims everything within 2–3h of Bergen, so anything genuinely new starts at Sogn, and nothing closer justifies a **US$365** rental when **Voss is 1h15 by direct train**.

## Sogndal — the base

**Bed:** [Sognefjord Bed & Waffle](https://www.booking.com/hotel/no/sognefjord-bed-amp-waffle.html), **Leirbakken 4** — private double, shared bath, shared kitchen, **free parking**, continental breakfast (batter delivered to the room, you make your own waffles). **2,250 NOK / 3n**, 8.7/57, location 9.1.

Sogndal is chosen as a **hub, not a sight** — it is the only bed in Sogn with every headline trail inside 1h15:

| To | Time | km |
|---|---:|---:|
| **Fjærland** (Flatbrehytta, Bøyabreen) | **0h37** | 37 |
| **Solvorn / Urnes** | **0h19** | 17 |
| **Molden** trailhead (Krossen) | ~0h45 | ~40 |
| **Balestrand** (Raudmelen) | **1h01** | 48 |

**In town, free first:** fjord swim off the Sogndalsfjøra waterfront · **heit Sogndal** floating sauna (paid, book ahead) · the **Anestølen / Sogndalsdalen** valley walk · **Stedjeåsen**, the local hill (AllTrails 4.3/97 — honestly the weakest trail of the week, which is exactly why you drive) · **Hodlekve** trail and bike centre up the valley.

**Kaupanger stave church**, 10 min east: **guided-tour season ends Aug 10**, so in September expect **exterior and churchyard only — free**. Don't build a day around the interior.

## Fjærland — glaciers and books

**Getting there:** Sogndal → Rv5 north through the Fjærland tunnel, **37 min / 37 km**, no ferry. Coming from Bergen you arrive from the opposite end via Skei, so Tuesday's stops are free.

| Do | Detail | Cost |
|---|---|---:|
| **Bøyabreen** ⭐ wet-day | AllTrails **4.1/85** · **1.4 km · 28 m · 30–60 min · easy** · forest path to a raised viewpoint on the glacier snout. Car park is on Rv5 just south of the tunnel | **free** |
| **Supphellebreen** ⭐ | Drive up **Supphelledalen** (~10 km north of Mundal) through farms to the valley car park with picnic tables · **under 5 minutes' walk** to the glacier toe and its meltwater river. One of the most accessible glaciers anywhere | **20 NOK/day parking** |
| **Flatbrehytta / Flatbreen** ⭐⭐ **Wed's hike** | AllTrails **4.7/175** · **8.2 km · ~1,000 m** · **2–4 h up, 2–3 h down** · challenging. **Same Supphelledalen car park.** Lush forest → moraine → rivers → avalanche paths → the cabin above Flatbreen, with Supphelledalen, the glacier and Fjærlandsfjorden all in one view | **free** *(+20 NOK parking)* |
| **The Norwegian Book Town** (Bokbyen), Mundal | **Open May–September** · ~**150,000 books** across ~10–12 outlets in converted ferry waiting rooms, stables, a bank, a post office and a grocery · thousands in English · **outdoor self-service shelves on the honesty system** | **free to browse** |
| **Norwegian Glacier Museum** (Norsk Bremuseum) | **September: daily 10:00–16:00** · panoramic Jostedalsbreen film, interactive climate exhibition, mammoths on the lawn, café | **180 NOK adult · 90 NOK student** |
| **Bøyaøyri nature reserve** | Protected estuary, ~100 bird species | **free** |

**Tuesday's wet-arrival sequence:** Bøyabreen (30 min, rain-proof under trees) → Mundal for the Book Town → Glacier Museum if it's still bucketing → tunnel to Sogndal.

## Balestrand — Thursday, the best day

**Getting there:** Sogndal → Leikanger → **Hella**, then the **Hella–Dragsvik ferry (10 min, 57 NOK EV)** → Balestrand. **1h01 / 48 km total.** There is no practical road-only alternative; the ferry is the route.

### Raudmelen — the corridor's best-rated hike

**AllTrails 4.9 / 199.** **Trailhead: Balestrand primary school at Kreklingen** — **15 minutes' walk from the quayside**, or 5 minutes to drive and park. **4 km one way · 80 m → 972 m · ~6 hours round trip · demanding.** Forest, then a long climb, then **stone steps** to a small hut at the summit. Free.

**Two honest bail-outs from the same information board at Kreklingen:**

| Turn round at | Height | Distance | Time | What you get |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| **Orrabenken** | **370 m** | ~2 km up | ~3 h round | **Picnic tables and benches** over the village and one of the widest stretches of the Sognefjord. An alternate descent line starts here |
| **Buråsi bench** | **575 m** | 5.4 km round | ~4 h | Panorama down Esedalen and over Esefjella; **Grauthaug** is the standout viewpoint just before the descent |

**Gate it on cloud.** 972 m inside the cloud base is a waste; Orrabenken at 370 m sits below most low cloud and is still a genuine view.

### Easier and other Balestrand options

| Walk | Start | Size | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Turstien** | Kreklingen | **5 km round · 200 m · easy** | Well-made path, bridges, part gravel — the rain option |
| **Esedalen** | Ese parking, 3 km toward Dragsvik | 3.5 km one way · 370 m | Untouched valley under **1,200 m cliffs**, old summer farm at Selbøen |
| Tjuatoten | Lunde parking, 9 km out | 4.1 km one way · **1,098 m** · 7 h | Ridge over Sognefjord, Esefjord, Vetlefjord and Fjærlandsfjord; old stone sun cross at the top. Too big for this trip |
| Keipen | Esebotn | 12 km · **1,426 m** · 10 h · expert | **Glacier traverse — no.** Invisible ice in late autumn |

**In the village:** **St Olaf's Church** (small English wooden church), the **King Bele statue** raised by Kaiser Wilhelm II, and the waterfront — all free. Note **Balestrand's mountain running race runs the Raudmelen route in mid-September**; check locally in case it closes the trail.

## Solvorn & Urnes — Friday morning

**Getting there:** Sogndal → **19 minutes / 17 km** to Solvorn. Then the **Urnes ferry** across Lustrafjord, 20 min.

- **Park in Solvorn and walk on as a foot passenger: 75 NOK** instead of **210 NOK** with the car. The church is a short uphill walk from Ornes quay, so the car adds nothing.
- **Friday departures from Solvorn: 07:10, then 10:00 and hourly to 16:50.** Tickets on board.
- **Urnes stave church** — Norway's oldest, **UNESCO**. **Open May 2 – Sept 30, 10:30–17:45 daily.** **150 NOK adult / 100 NOK student.** **Entry is only on the guided tour**, which is included and runs **every 15–20 minutes**.
- **Solvorn** itself: white wooden houses on the water, a beach you can swim off, lunch on the shore. Free.

**Friday timing:** 10:00 ferry out, 11:00 or 12:00 back, on the road by 12:30, Bergen ~16:45 — comfortably inside **Kulturnatt, 18:00–00:00**.

## Luster & Jostedal — the swap options

Not in the default four days, but each is a legitimate substitution.

| Do | Getting there | Detail | Cost |
|---|---|---|---:|
| **Molden** ⭐ best swap | **Krossen car park**, ~45 min from Sogndal · **61.33319, 7.2720** · **free parking**, flush toilets, seasonal kiosk · no overnight parking 00:00–06:00 | **8.6 km · 620 m · ~4 h · DNT blue/moderate** · **529 AllTrails reviews** · red paint and signs, stone steps, boardwalks over wet ground · summit **1,116 m** with cairn and stone shelter · views into Lustrafjord and out to **Jotunheimen and Jostedalsbreen** | **free** |
| **Nigardsbreen** | 1h08 from Sogndal, then the **glacier toll road** — automated barrier, pay by card | Walk the shore of **Nigardsbrevatnet** to the ice front over rock slabs, ~45 min each way. **This is the free version.** The lake boat runs roughly June → **Oct 1**, water-level dependent | **90 NOK toll**, walk free |
| Breheimsenteret | At the foot of the glacier road | Visitor centre; also the booking desk for guided ice | **65–95 NOK** |
| Guided glacier walk | Book via Breheimsenteret | **3–6 hours** on the ice | **570–1,450 NOK** |
| **Feigefossen** | ~1h17 from Sogndal | **218 m — Norway's second-highest unregulated waterfall** · ~45 min walk up | **free** |
| **Sognefjellsvegen** | Turtagrø 1h13 from Sogndal | Norway's highest mountain pass road, National Tourist Route. **Scenic drive, 2–9 °C up top** — a drive, not a day | **free** |

**If you only add one:** Molden. It is the third-best trail in the region on review volume, the parking is free, and it is closer than Nigardsbreen.
