# Gothenburg — food, nature, sights, lodging & transport
*Good length **2 nights** (3 to add a Vrångö archipelago day; 0 = skip on a long Copenhagen → Oslo day) · nature-first · prices seen Jul–Aug 2026 at ~10.5 SEK ≈ $1*
*Other Scandinavian cities and the transit spine: [scandi-transit.md](scandi-transit.md)*

## How long

| Nights | Do |
|---|---|
| **0** | Skip — Copenhagen → Oslo in one long day |
| **2** | Arrival afternoon: Haga + Skansen Kronan + fika · Full day: Botaniska → Änggårdsbergen · eat räkmacka + köttbullar · leave next morning |
| **3** | Add a full **Vrångö** island day; keep Haga for an evening |

| Idea | 2n | 3n |
|---|---|---|
| Haga + Skansen Kronan | yes | yes |
| Botaniska + Änggårdsbergen | yes | optional if doing the island day |
| **Vrångö** | usually no | yes |
| Volvo / Universeum / Liseberg | no | no |
| Ramberget sunset | if energy | if energy |

Late August: sunset ~20:30–20:45; swimming fine on warm days.

## Getting around

### Västtrafik Zone A (prices Aug 2026)
| Ticket | SEK | ~USD | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single (~90 min) | 36 | ~$3.40 | 1–2 rides |
| **24h** | 115 | ~$11 | Botaniska day or many trams |

App: **Västtrafik To Go** (trams, buses, archipelago ferries). Long distance: **SJ** (from Copenhagen), **Vy** / Entur (to Oslo), FlixBus.

### Arrive / leave
| Leg | Mode | Time | ~USD (2026) | Stations |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| From Copenhagen | Train | 3.5–4h | $25–45 | København H → Göteborg C |
| To Oslo | Train | 3.5–4h | $30–50 | Göteborg C → Oslo S |
| To Oslo | **FlixBus** | 3h35 | ~$36 | Nils Ericsonterminalen → Oslo bussterminal (e.g. 08:50 → 12:25, Aug 2026) |

Allow 30–40 min at the station.

## Sample 2-night plan

| Day | Focus | Meals |
|---|---|---|

### Late-August events
- **Göteborgskalaset** — free city festival in the last week of August (27–30 Aug in 2026) with stages at Götaplatsen, Kungstorget, Bältespännarparken (local beer + dance zone) and the Packhuskajen harbour festival by Göteborgsoperan (food trucks, covered seating). [Program](https://goteborgskalaset.se/programmet).
- **Gothenburg Fringe** opens around the same time at Stora Tullhuset, with free drop-in events.
- Big stadium concerts at Ullevi happen the same week: umbrellas banned, small bags only.

## Where to stay

| Area | Why |
|---|---|
| **Inom Vallgraven** | Station + Saluhallen + walkable core |
| **Linné / Haga edge** | Fika, pubs, Haga on foot · tram to station |
| **Lorensberg / Heden** | Value hotels · park edge |

**Skip:** Waterfront Cabins (across the river — not walkable at night) · Barken Viking (~7.3, mixed maintenance). Generator has no Gothenburg branch.

## Money savers (Sweden)
*Country rules: [scandi-money-tips.md](scandi-money-tips.md)*

| Do | Skip / watch |
|---|---|
| **Willys / Lidl** + hostel kitchen | ICA/Coop for every staple |
| **Dagens lunch** ~$11–14 | Full dinner menus at midday |
| Kåges / Saluhallen plates | Waterfront tourist set menus |
| **Free:** Botaniska, Änggårdsbergen, Haga, Slottsskogen, Trädgårdsföreningen / Palmhuset | **Liseberg** on a short stop (~$45+ entry) |
| Västtrafik 24h on the Botaniska day | Taxis |
| Älvsnabbare harbour commuter boats (often free or cheap) | Paid tourist boats on the same water |
| Archipelago ferries on a normal Västtrafik ticket | "Island tour" packages |
| Systembolaget for wine / strong beer (check hours) | Bar rounds every night ($8–12/beer) |
| Tap water | Bottled water |
