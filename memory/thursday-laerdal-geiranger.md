## ⚠️ Before you go

- **Tindevegen is a private toll road and seasonal.** If shut, go **west via Stryn** — 246 km / 4h24, nearly the same time, but you lose Sognefjellet *and* Lom.
- **Sognefjellet is open** — Statens vegvesen's closure fields are blank. 108 km, 0–1,434 m.
- **Dalsnibba closed 9 Sep after snowfall.** Check `geirangerskywalk.no`. **Ørnesvingen is open year-round** and carries the classic view, so the day works either way.
- **Sub-zero at altitude, but dry:** Grotli **−2.6°C**, Tindevegen −1.1°C with **45% fog until ~09:00**, Djupvasshytta −0.8°C. Black-ice caution, not weather. A 06:30 start means hitting Tindevegen as the fog lifts.
