# Oslo — food, nature, sights, lodging & transport
*Good length **3 nights** (2 = drop Sognsvann + one museum; 4 = add sauna + deeper Nordmarka) · nature-first · prices seen Jul–Aug 2026 at ~10.5–11 NOK ≈ $1*
*Other Scandinavian cities and the transit spine: [scandi-transit.md](scandi-transit.md) · heading south-west by rail: [Oslo → Stavanger stopovers](oslo-stavanger-stopovers.md)*

## How long

| Nights | Do |
|---|---|
| **2** | Opera + Akershus + Ekeberg · Vigeland + Grünerløkka · **one of** Munch or the National Museum · Hovedøya or skip · eat at Schrøder + Fiskeriet |
| **3** | The full plan below |
| **4** | Add a harbour **sauna** and/or **Vettakollen** / longer Nordmarka · optional second museum or Bygdøy on a wet day |

- Free parks and islands first; pick **one** big museum; eat husmannskost, not Aker Brygge.
- Emanuel Vigeland Mausoleum opens only on Sundays.
- Late August: long evenings for Ekeberg. **Fårikål** season starts in September and may show up as late-August specials. If Preikestolen is next, save your legs.

## Sample 3-night plan

| Day | Nature / sights | Food |
|---|---|---|
| Arrival afternoon | **Opera roof** · Akershus ramparts | Pølse or a Mathallen graze |
| Day 1 | **Munch** (timed ticket) or easy waterfront · **Ekebergparken** at sunset | **Schrøder** (flesk og duppe / kjøttkaker) |
| Day 2 | Metro → **Sognsvann** loop · **Vigeland** · Akerselva → **Grünerløkka** | **Kaffistova** dagens · Syverkiosken snack · Grünerløkka / Mathallen dinner |
| Day 3 | Ferry to **Hovedøya** (ruins + woods) · **Mathallen** · Bygdøy if raining | **Fiskeriet** takeaway fish soup on Youngstorget |

Two nights: drop Sognsvann, shorten Hovedøya, one museum. Four nights: floating sauna + Vettakollen or a deeper Nordmarka day.

## Food

Oslo is expensive: husmannskost cafeterias and takeaway fish beat harbour terraces. Some classics sit at ~4.0–4.4★ but win on value and portion size.

### 3-night eat plan
| When | Eat |
|---|---|
| Dinner | **Schrøder** — flesk og duppe or kjøttkaker |
| Dinner / lunch | **Fiskeriet** — creamy fish soup (**takeaway is cheaper**) on Youngstorget |
| One meal | **Kaffistova** — kjøttkaker / raspeballer (Thursdays) / rømmegrøt · dagens |
| Snack | **Syverkiosken** — pølse i lompe |
| Market | **Vulkanfisk** at Mathallen — soup or fiskegrateng |
| Anytime | Samson / Baker Hansen kanelbolle · Kaffebrenneriet filter coffee |

### Laundry near Frogner (Aug 2026)
- **[Clean Cocos Frogner](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Clean+Cocos+Skovveien+6+Oslo)**, Skovveien 6 (~14 min walk from Cochs). Open daily 06:00–22:00, unstaffed. Six washers (8 kg) and six dryers; **79 NOK** per wash and **79 NOK** per dry (158 NOK total), detergent auto-dosed and included. Wash ~60 min; empty the lint filter before drying. Google 3.8/115: machines work, but the room is tiny and crowded on Sunday afternoons and the app sometimes fails. **Pay with the contactless card terminal**, not the Appwash app (which needs a prepaid top-up). Bring a wipe; stay with your clothes. Norway support line +47 33 39 89 43.
- **Café Laundromat**, Underhaugsveien 2 (~9 min from Cochs): a staffed café with 4 washers and only 2 dryers, 10 NOK coins (change at the bar); reports of clothes still wet after an hour. Go for the café, not reliable laundry.
