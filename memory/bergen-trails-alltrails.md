### Why each

- **Løvstien** — wide gravel path along Løvstakken's base, Melkeplassen → Langeskogen, only 181 m of climb, with a 383 m pedestrian bridge clinging to the cliffside and six "Nature Rooms" along the way. Low gain and hard surface make it the one trail that genuinely works in 22 mm of rain.
- **Montana – Ulriken via Oppstemten** — starts at the hostel door. Sherpa staircase to the summit; technically easy but steep, last kilometre over 20%. Shorter loop variant: [Montana–Oppstemten–Ulriken rundtur](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/norway/vestland/montana-oppstemten-ulriken-rundtur), 4.3 km · 431 m · 4.6 (131).
- **Blåmanen Loop from Fløyen** — best rating *and* most reviews of anything under 8 km in Bergen. Varied terrain, views over the city, the surrounding ranges, the fjords and the sea. Starts and ends at the **Fløibanen top station**, so it needs an access leg (below). AllTrails publishes no estimated time; at 7.1 km / 367 m moderate, budget **~2.5–3 hr**.
  - **Access, 8 km-strict:** funicular up and down — exactly 7.1 km walked, 210–240 NOK.
  - **Access, recommended:** funicular up (105–120 NOK), loop, then **walk down Fløysvingene** (3.2 km, easy graded descent) into the centre. Total 10.3 km walked but the extra is all downhill, and it puts you 1.1 km from Grieghallen.
  - **Access, free:** walk up Fløysvingene too — 13.5 km total, which is back in Vidden territory.
  - Rough timing: up ~10:00 · loop to ~13:00–13:30 · down by ~15:00. Clear before the 18:00 talk and the 19:30 opera.
  - *Note:* AllTrails' description calls Blåmanen one of Bergen's seven mountains. It isn't — the canonical seven are Sandviksfjellet, Fløyfjellet, Rundemanen, Ulriken, Løvstakken, Damsgårdsfjellet and Lyderhorn. Good trail regardless.
- **Krohnegården – Løvstakken** — deliberately short so Kulturnatt survives. 360° over the North Sea and the vidda behind Ulriken. **Return the same way** — descending the far side lands you in the wrong valley.
- **Strandkaien – Fløyen** — from the harbour past the Fish Market, up paved and gravel paths through birch and rhododendron. Short enough to be done and dusted before the 17:00 handoff.
