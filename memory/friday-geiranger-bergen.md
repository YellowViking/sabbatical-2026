## 🏞 Gaularfjellet — why it's worth two extra hours

**National Scenic Route · 114 km · 0–784 m · Moskog ↔ Balestrand · opened 1938 · OPEN** *(Statens vegvesen closure fields blank)*

> *"The Gaula river has **29 large waterfalls**, with **Likholefossen and the bridge across it** as one of the highlights. A walk over the exciting bridge gives you a sense of being **in the midst of the swirling cascades**."*
> *"A peaceful alternative to the main roads that absorb most of the traffic."*

Stops: **Likholefossen · Hestad · Torsnesstølen · Utsikten**
Also: the **21 km Fossestien Waterfall Trail** runs along the Gaula parallel to the road — walkable in short sections.
