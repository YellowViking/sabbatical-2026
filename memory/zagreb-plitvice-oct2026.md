# Zagreb + Plitvice: Sun 18 → Tue 20 Oct 2026

*2 nights in Zagreb. Plitvice as a **day trip on Mon 19**, because Plitvice is a dead end: every through route is longer than going there and back. The older general Croatia notes are in [croatia-local-food.md](croatia-local-food.md).*

## Arrive (Sun 18)

**D 315 Lesce-Bled 13:29 → Ljubljana 14:14, then INT 211 14:45 → Zagreb Glavni kolodvor 17:23** (1 change, 31 min).

Tickets:
- SŽ, Bled → Ljubljana: €5.80, −30% at weekends.
- SŽ "Zagreb Spezial", Ljubljana → Zagreb: €9, bought at the Ljubljana counter.
- ÖBB's through fare is €35.40.

**LP on Zagreb:** "fun to visit year-round". Evening walk: Upper Town, Ban Jelačić Square, the blue trams. (Lead: Zagreb Open House runs 16–18 Oct.)

## Plitvice day trip (Mon 19)

| | Time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| FlixBus Zagreb → Plitvice | **06:45 → 09:00** (or 08:00 → 10:22) | €16.99 |
| Park | Entrances 07:00–18:00; **ticket sales until 15:00**; boats and panoramic trains running | **€23** in October (official; €10 from 1 Nov). Buy online |
| FlixBus Plitvice → Zagreb | **16:15 → 18:37** (or 17:15 → 19:05) | €16.99 |

That gives about 7 hours in the park, enough for the main lake loop.

- **Mid-to-late October is peak autumn colour, with crowds down 70–80%** (lead).
- The upper lakes can close early in November if there's frost.

**⚠️ Weather:** the forecast for Mon 19 is **9/3 °C with 1.2 mm**: cold, light drizzle. Take layers. If it turns properly wet, do Plitvice on **Tue 20** and start Italy on **Wed 21**.
