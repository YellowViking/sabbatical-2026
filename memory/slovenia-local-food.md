# Slovenia — Ljubljana + Julian Alps
*Default **7n** · flex **6–9n** · after Vilnius flight · before Croatia · nature-first · euro · early Oct · AI Mode Pro + Maps check · Aug 2026*

**Shape:** **split base** — ~**3n Ljubljana** + ~**4n Bled or Bohinj** (not seven city nights). Soča full day is a stretch without a car.  
**Arrive:** VNO → LJU flight · **Leave:** train/bus → Zagreb.  
**Money / transit indexes:** Nordic habits still apply (tap water, lunch specials); local apps below.

| **City LJU** | Urbana / local tickets · walk Old Town |

| **Ride** | Bolt sparingly |
| **Caves** | Arriva/Nomago to Postojna · train Divača + park shuttle for Škocjan |

### Arrive / leave
| Leg | Mode | ~USD | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **VNO → LJU** | Flight connect | ~$90–180 | Airport bus ~€4 to station — skip €35 taxi |
| **LJU → Bled** | Arriva bus | ~$6–12 | ~1–1.5h |
| **Bled → Bohinj** | Arriva | ~$5–10 | Hourly-ish in season |
| **LJU → Zagreb** | EC train or FlixBus | ~$15–40 | Prefer train/bus over private shuttle |
