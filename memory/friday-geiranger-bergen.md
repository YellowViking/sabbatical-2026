## ⚠️ Three things to know

**1 · The Dragsvik–Vangsnes ferry is unverified.** It's part of the Hella–Dragsvik–Vangsnes "Sognefjord triangle" — a local public crossing, not a tourist boat, so it should be nothing like the **NOK 1,680** quoted for Geiranger–Hellesylt. But I could not confirm its timetable or price. **The whole 38-minute saving depends on it.** Check before Balestrand.

**2 · Vikafjellet will be at dusk.** Sunset ~19:30; you'd be crossing ~18:00–19:00. Doable, but the alpine plateau is the payoff and you'd see it in failing light. Leaving Geiranger at **09:30** (dropping Flydalsjuvet) buys 90 minutes.

**3 · It goes through Voss** — ~20 min transit, not a stop. Vik's only southern road is Vikafjellet, and Vikafjellet's only outlet is Voss. Unavoidable if you want this route.

**Bail-outs:** at **Byrkjelo (~14:00)** stay on the E39 → Bergen 18:50. At **Balestrand (~16:45)**, if the ferry doesn't work, continue Høyanger → Lavik → Bergen.
