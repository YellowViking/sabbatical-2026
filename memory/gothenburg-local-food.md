### Feskekôrka note
Beautiful “Fish Church” — go for the building. Post-renovation stalls skew pricier (**Gabriel closed**). Prefer Sjöbaren / Fiskbar 17 for value.

**Book food:** Sjöbaren dinner if busy. **Walk-in:** Kåges lunch, Fiskbar 17, fika.  
**Dagens lunch** weekdays ~11–14 often ~$12–15 incl. salad/bread/coffee.

## Lodging *(hostels + budget)*

*Criteria match itinerary §7: review **>8**, high count, **central**, social OK. Late-Aug ballpark · ~10.5 SEK≈$1 · free cancel while nights flex 0–3. AI Mode Pro Aug 2026 — re-check Booking.*

### Where to stay
| Area | Why |
|---|---|
| **Inom Vallgraven** | Station + Saluhallen + walk-everywhere core |
| **Linné / Haga edge** | Fika, pubs, short walk Haga · tram to station |

**⚠ Skip:** **Waterfront Cabins** (across river — not walkable at night) · **Barken Viking** (~7.3, mixed maintenance) · **Generator** (no GOT branch — Stockholm only).

### How to choose
| You want | Book |
|---|---|
| Social dorm + **curtains** / cook | **Slottsskogens** |
| Quiet dorm, curtains less critical | STF (open bunks) |
| Private value | **Hotell Heden** or Le Mat |
| Walk-off-train luxury-ish | **Hotel Royal** |
| Only 0–1n glue stop | **Inom Vallgraven** (Royal/Heden) — less tram tax |
| 3n + Vrångö day | Slottsskogens OK; early tram to boat links |

**Budget:** dorms here are a steal vs Oslo/CPH (~**$20–25**). Private value ~**$90–110**; Royal ~$150–190 if you want zero friction after CPH train.
