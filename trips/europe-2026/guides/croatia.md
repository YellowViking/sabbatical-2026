# Croatia: Zagreb, Plitvice, Split, Dubrovnik

Reference for an inland-plus-coast Croatia trip. The **Zagreb** section was checked on the ground in October 2026 (own-menu prices, beer prices, hostel). The **Plitvice, Split and Dubrovnik** sections are pre-trip desk research from August 2026 (AI Mode + Google Maps); re-check them before relying on them. Croatia uses the euro.

**Suggested shape** for a nature-first trip of about 2 weeks: 2–3 nights Zagreb (city + a Medvednica day) · Plitvice as a day trip from Zagreb, or 1 night by the park for a dawn start · 5 nights Split (Marjan, Trogir, a Hvar day) · 3–4 nights Dubrovnik (walls, Srđ hike). Skip Krka if you've done Plitvice. Mid-October is shoulder season: warm coast, fewer crowds, thinner ferries.

Related: [slovenia-local-food.md](slovenia.md) (Ljubljana, Bled, Julian Alps) · [graz-local-food.md](graz.md) · [scandi-transit.md](scandinavia-transit.md) · [scandi-money-tips.md](scandinavia-money.md).

**Cross-border rail tickets (Croatia ↔ Slovenia) are sold only at station counters.** Each operator's e-shop sells domestic tickets only. In Zagreb Glavni kolodvor the **HŽ international windows** are in the corridor to the left of the main hall (open daily 05:20–21:40); ask for the "Ljubljana Spezial" one-way. Platforms aren't published online: find the train on the *Odlasci* (departures) board (*kolosijek* = track). R 432 is a regional train with no buffet, so buy food first. Keep your passport handy at the Dobova border (Schengen, but spot checks happen).

**ÖBB through fares can mislead:** its "€10.60 Zagreb → Lesce-Bled" was in fact Ljubljana → Lesce-Bled only (the offer page said "Ticket valid for section of the journey only").

## Zagreb

### Sights and nature
- **Gornji Grad** (Upper Town) and **Donji Grad** (Lower Town grid), Ban Jelačić square, **Dolac market**, **Mirogoj cemetery**, the Museum of Broken Relationships (optional).
- The **17:00 free walking tour** plus the **lamplighter** in the Upper Town at dusk.
- **Medvednica / Sljeme** for a forest day: tram toward Mihaljevac, then bus (or the cable car) to Sljeme; Medvedgrad trails.

- **Ficlek** (4.7, 3,669 reviews), behind Dolac market: the best value overall. Also grenadirmarš (potato and pasta) €6.90, bučnica (pumpkin strudel) €4.40.
- **La Štruk** (4.6, 9,531), Skalinska 5: the štrukli specialist; deep-fried štrukli bites €6.
- **Batak Tkalčićeva** (4.6, 3,293): ćevapi with cheese €6.90 (5) or €8.90 (7); gourmet pljeskavica €12.50.
- **Stari Fijaker 900** (about 4.5): štrukli and turkey with mlinci, €7–15 (Aug 2026 research).
- **Mlinar** and other *pekara* bakeries: burek or a pizza slice for breakfast, €2–4.

## Plitvice Lakes

- **Day trip from Zagreb by FlixBus** (no train): 06:45 → 09:00, back 16:15 → 18:37; €16.99 each way (Oct 2026).
- **Park ticket €23 in October** (official, 2026); ticket sales end at 15:00. Buy online; entry is timed. Program C is about 8 km.
- Plitvice is a dead end: every through route (to Split, Rijeka) is longer than an out-and-back from Zagreb. Plitvice → Rijeka is a 5½ h bus.
- Mid-to-late October is peak autumn colour (lead). Upper lakes can close early in November if there's frost.
- **Sleeping by the park** for a dawn start (Aug 2026 research): **Falling Lakes Hostel** (Korenica; Maps 4.7, Hostelworld 9.9 from 2.6k; kitchen; morning guest shuttle to Entrance 2, reconfirm it) or **House Tina** (Grabovac; 4.8; private about $70–100; not walk-to-gate). For a true walk-in dawn start, look in **Rastovača** (Entrance 1) or **Mukinje** (Entrance 2).

## Split (Aug 2026 research)

- **Sights:** Diocletian's Palace cellars and peristyle early or late, the Riva, Varoš lanes. Avoid hosts with laminated menus at the palace gates.
- **Nature:** **Marjan Hill** (free loops from Varoš, Telegrin views, 2–4 h); **Trogir** (UNESCO island core) by bus 37, half a day; **Hvar** day by Jadrolinija ferry with the Fortica fort and coast path.
- **Food:** **Villa Spiza** (about 4.6): crni rižot (black risotto), market catch, €12–18. Green market + konoba.
- **Stay:** Varoš, Radunica, the walkable Old Town edge; avoid the palace party belt and far-east Duilovo.

## Dubrovnik (Aug 2026 research)

- **Walls** once, bundled in the **Dubrovnik Pass** (walls + museums + local bus); side streets off Stradun; Lapad promenade.
- **Mount Srđ:** hike the switchbacks from above the Old Town (1.5–2.5 h, free) instead of the cable car.
- **Lokrum:** boat from the Old Town, half a day, if running. **Pelješac / Ston** oysters as an extra day.
- **Food:** **Konoba Kamenice** (about 4.5): Ston oysters, *dagnje na buzaru* (mussels), €3–15.
- **Stay** in Lapad / Babin Kuk (swim, bus 4/6, better for 3–4 nights). Old Town beds mean stairs with a pack; Buža bars are pricey.

## Money savers
