## 1. Spain — Non-Lucrative Visa (NLV)

### What it is
Spanish national residence visa for foreigners who will **reside without engaging in labor or professional activity**. Consulates and guides treat **remote work for a foreign employer as professional activity → reject / renew risk**.

### Money (IPREM math — know this)

Spain pegs the floor to **IPREM** (public income indicator). 2026 discourse still uses roughly:

| | Multiple | € / year (if IPREM €600/mo = €7,200/yr) | ~USD |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Main applicant** | **400% IPREM** | **€28,800** | ~**$32,000** |
| **Each dependent** | **+100% IPREM** | **+€7,200** | ~**+$8,000** |

**How people actually prove it**
- Last **~3 months** bank / brokerage statements showing **liquid** funds  
- Often **tax return**  
- Passive income (pension, dividends) helps the story but **lump savings are commonly used**  
- **Show more than the floor** (many advisors say 1.5–2× comfort) — consulates hate “exactly €28,800 and a mortgage eating cashflow”  
- Heavy US **debt** can hurt optics even if assets are large  

**Renewal money:** renewals often expect means covering the **next multi-year block** (e.g. **2×** annual bar for a 2-year renewal — confirm current instruction). Don’t spend down to the bone in year 1.

### Documents (typical US file)

| Doc | Notes |
|---|---|
| National visa form + photo + passport | Valid well beyond stay |
| **FBI** identity history (and/or state checks per consulate) | Fresh enough (often **&lt; ~6 months**); **apostille**; **sworn Spanish translation** |
| **Private health insurance** | Insurer **authorized in Spain**; **full coverage**; guides stress **no copays / no waiting periods**; repatriation often expected |
| **Financials** | Statements, investments, proof of origin of funds if asked |
| **Housing in Spain** | Lease or deed / booking that covers **initial months** (often **≥3 months** story); need a Spanish address for **empadronamiento** |
| **Notarized affidavit** | Who you are, why Spain, **explicit no work / no remote work** |
| Consular fee + copies | Jurisdiction-specific (NY, DC, LA, Miami, etc. or BLS) |

### Process timeline (planning sketch)

```text
T−3–4 mo   FBI fingerprints → apostille → translations
T−2–3 mo   Spain-compliant insurance quote/bind · housing search
T−2 mo     Consulate/BLS appointment packet
T−1–3 mo   Wait (varies wildly by post)
Approval   Visa sticker ~**90 days to enter**
Landing    ≤~30 days: **padrón** (town hall) → **TIE** appointment (police)
Year 1     Live · keep funds · don’t work · track absences
Renew      1 → 2 → 2 year pattern commonly described → PR discussion ~5y
```

### After arrival
- **TIE** = physical residence card — do this early; appointments can lag  
- **Padrón** = local registration — needed for life admin  
- **Other Schengen:** with valid residence, short trips usually under **90/180** rules as a resident traveler — **Spain must remain your main home** for renewals (long continuous absence can kill renewal; **~6 months** consecutive abroad is a commonly cited danger zone)

### Tax (do not DIY)
- Spending **≥183 days** in Spain in a calendar year generally makes you **Spanish tax resident**  
- Worldwide income / wealth concepts may apply; **Beckham law is not** the NLV product  
- US citizens: **still US tax persons** — plan **FTC**, FBAR, maybe forms for foreign accounts **before** you move  
- Hire a pro who has done **US + Spain NLV** clients

### Where to live (nature-first, cost-aware)

| Area | Nature | Winter | Cost vibe | English / vibe |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Asturias** (Oviedo, Gijón edge) | **Picos de Europa**, green cliffs, Atlantic | Wet, cool | **Lower** | Spanish; fewer tourists inland |
| **Galicia** (Santiago, A Coruña, smaller rías towns) | Forests, coast, Camino energy | Wet, Atlantic | **Lower–mid** | Spanish/Galician |
| **Cantabria** | Mountains + coast | Wet | Lower–mid | Quieter |
| **Valencia** (city or smaller towns inland/coast) | Med light, Albufera, nearby hills | Mild winters | **Mid–high** in city | More expat infrastructure |
| **Andalusia** | Sun, mountains (Sierra Nevada) | Mild | Varies | Touristy pockets |
| **Barcelona / Madrid** | City + day-trip nature | OK | **High** housing fight | Best English — worst NLV value |

**Monthly burn excl. rent (very rough):** Green North ~€700–950 · Valencia area ~€950–1,300 · add rent €600–1,400+ by city/size.  
**All-in comfortable solo:** often **~$2.0–3.2k/mo** outside premium cores if you don’t live like a tourist daily.

## 2. France — Long-stay visitor (VLS-T / “visiteur”)

### What it is
French **visa de long séjour** mentioning **visiteur**: stay in France **more than 90 days** as a visitor **without working**. Same philosophical box as NLV; different bureaucracy brand (France-Visas, OFII/validation, préfecture renewals).

### Money
No single pretty IPREM table in popular English guides. Practice clusters around:

| Idea | Ballpark |
|---|---|
| **Monthly means** | About **French minimum wage (SMIC)** order of magnitude — often cited **~€1,400–1,600+/mo** equivalent |
| **Annualized** | ~**€17–20k+** as a *conversation floor* — **weak** if that’s all you show |
| **Savings** | Large liquid balance + explanation of drawdown **can** work; consulates want a **sustainable year** story |
| **Practical file** | Show **comfortably above** SMIC (many successful files look like **€2k–3k+/mo** equivalent lifestyle + buffer) |

**Proof:** bank statements, investment summaries, sometimes attestation of support; consistency matters more than one lucky screenshot.

### Documents (typical US file)

| Doc | Notes |
|---|---|
| **France-Visas** account + form | Start online; print packet |
| Passport, photos, fee | — |
| **Criminal record** | US FBI / state as required; apostille + **certified French translation** often |
| **Health insurance** | Full cover for France for full stay; medical repatriation; check if consulate wants specific ceilings (€30k+ Schengen is tourist-grade — long-stay often wants **stronger**) |
| **Means of support** | Statements, portfolio, pension letters |
| **Housing** | Bail (lease), deed, or **attestation d’hébergement** + host ID if staying with someone; hotel-only is weaker for a year story |
| **Purpose letter** | Why France, ties, **no professional activity in France** |
| Proof of US residence / ties | Sometimes requested |

### Process timeline (planning sketch)

```text
T−3 mo     France-Visas draft · FBI · translations · insurance
T−2 mo     VFS / consulate biometrics appointment
T−1–3 mo   Decision wait (post-dependent)
Approval   VLS-T sticker (often **“carte de séjour à solliciter”** style instructions — read the visa)
Landing    **Validate** long-stay visa (OFII / online portal — **deadlines are real**)
Months 8–12  Préfecture **renewal** / titre de séjour visitor (appointments = pain point)
```

French **préfecture** appointment scarcity is a known quality-of-life tax. Budget time and patience.

### After arrival
- **Validation** of VLS-T is mandatory early — missing it = illegal stay risk  
- Day-to-day: social security is **not** “free healthcare because visa” — you stay on **private insurance** until/unless another status  
- Travel in Schengen: possible under residence/visitor rules; **France should remain your base**  
- Renewals: prove still means-tested, still not working, still housed

### Tax (do not DIY)
- **183 days** or **main home / center of economic interest** can create **French tax residency**  
- Worldwide income exposure + US citizenship compliance — same warning as Spain  
- France has its own social/tax complexity; get a **US–France** aware advisor before month 6

### Where to live (nature-first, cost-aware)

| Area | Nature | Winter | Cost vibe | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Annecy** | Lake + Alps trails | Cold, gorgeous | **High** for FR nature trophy | Tourist rents |
| **Pau / Basque-Pyrenees edge** | Pyrenees access, green | Milder than Alps | **Mid** | Excellent sabbatical shape |
| **Grenoble / Chambéry edge** | Alps | Cold | Mid | More “real city” |
| **Brittany** (Rennes edge, coast towns) | Wild coast, hiking | Wet, windy | Mid | Moody Atlantic — like Green Spain cousin |
| **Lyon** | City + day nature | Cold-ish | Mid–high | Food; less pure nature |
| **Paris** | Parks only | Gray | **Very high** | Skip as NLV/VLS-T value play |
| **Occitanie / smaller south** | Hills, some Med | Mild | Mid | Check train links |

**All-in comfortable solo:** often **~$2.3–3.8k/mo** depending on Annecy-vs-Pau; Paris can blow past **$4k+**.

### France — fit score for you

| Lens | Score | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Savings-only | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Works; less “one number” than IPREM |
