## Sun 15: Monte Veneretta + Taormina walk
- **Getting up there:** from Villa Maria, walk about 10 min to the *Villagonia Via Nazionale* stop by the station. Take Interbus **IN09 at 09:15** to Taormina, then **IN10** to Castelmola (arrives 10:05). Alternatively, IN10 direct at 09:11. Later IN10 buses up: 10:11, 11:11.
- **Hike:** [Castelmola – Monte Veneretta](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/italy/sicily/castelmola-monte-veneretta). AllTrails 4.7 (58 reviews), 6.1 km out and back, 430 m of climbing, 2.5–3 h, no shade.
- **Lunch:** PierdeCat (4.9) or Bar Turrisi (almond wine) in Castelmola.
- **Walking down (LP):** Saraceni path → Porta Messina → Palazzo Corvaja → Piazza IX Aprile (cannolo from D'Amore) → Torre dell'Orologio → Palazzo Duca di Santo Stefano → Villa Comunale → Belvedere di Via Pirandello → **Mazzarò steps** (700+ steps, 45–60 min) → Via Nazionale → flat to Villa Maria.
  ▶ [Route in Google Maps (walking)](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/Bar+Turrisi,+Castelmola/Porta+Messina,+Taormina/Palazzo+Corvaja,+Taormina/Piazza+IX+Aprile,+Taormina/Torre+dell+Orologio,+Taormina/Palazzo+Duca+di+Santo+Stefano,+Taormina/Villa+Comunale+di+Taormina/Belvedere+di+Via+Pirandello,+Taormina/Sentiero+Taormina+Isola+Bella,+Taormina/B%26B+Villa+Maria,+Vico+Manganelli,+Giardini-Naxos/data=!4m2!4m1!3e2)
- **Bus down instead of walking:** IN10 from *Castelmola Traversa Tutti I Santi* at 13:09, 15:09 or 16:09.
- **Closed right now:** the Mazzarò cable car (9 Nov–8 Dec, replacement shuttle hourly) and Isola Bella island (until about 15 Dec). Teatro Antico closes at 16:00 in winter.

Villa Maria → station is 600 m, 8 min on the flat. Buy in the Trenitalia app.

**In Cefalù:**
- **Bag:** Bounce (travel agency, €3.50, 09:00–21:00, 6 min from the station) or Radical Storage's Old Town office (€3.90).
- **Rocca:** winter hours 08:00–17:00, **last entry 15:00** (official site visitcefalu.com). LP gives €5; the official site sells only a €10 combined pass. Up via Vicolo Saraceni, 1.5 km, 240 m of climbing.
- **Rocks walk:** from Porto Vecchio go through the jetty arch → Bastione Capo Marchiafava (sunset 16:46) → Capo Cefalù lighthouse.
  ▶ [Cefalù walk (walking)](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/Stazione+di+Cefal%C3%B9/Vicolo+Saraceni,+Cefal%C3%B9/Tempio+di+Diana,+Cefal%C3%B9/Spiaggia+del+Porto+Vecchio,+Cefal%C3%B9/Bastione+di+Capo+Marchiafava,+Cefal%C3%B9/Faro+di+Capo+Cefal%C3%B9/Stazione+di+Cefal%C3%B9/data=!4m2!4m1!3e2)
- **Food (open Mondays):** La Cefalù Nostrana (4.9), Tinchité, Le Chat Noir (lunch 12:30–14:00), Duomo Gelatieri dal 1952.

**🚲 Bike loop (Tue 17):**
- Rent from **Palermo on Bike / Social Bike**, Discesa dei Giudici 13: **€15 for the day** (€10 for up to 4 h).
- Route: **along the coast both ways.** Foro Italico → Arenella → the Addaura road under Monte Pellegrino → Mondello → **Capo Gallo lighthouse** (19.5 km, about 1h04), then back the same way. **39.7 km in total, about 2h14.**
  ▶ [Coastal bike route in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/Palermo+on+Bike,+Discesa+dei+Giudici+13,+Palermo/Foro+Italico,+Palermo/Arenella,+Palermo/Addaura,+Palermo/Piazza+Mondello,+Palermo/Il+Faro+Capo+Gallo,+Palermo/Piazza+Mondello,+Palermo/Addaura,+Palermo/Arenella,+Palermo/Foro+Italico,+Palermo/Palermo+on+Bike,+Discesa+dei+Giudici+13,+Palermo/data=!4m2!4m1!3e1)
