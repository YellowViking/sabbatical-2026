# Sicily guide

Reference for a budget, solo, car-free trip around eastern and northern Sicily in late autumn, built from research and travel in Nov 2026. The mainland is in [italy-guide.md](italy.md); what actually happened on the 2026 trip is in [../trip-log.md](../README.md).

Picks follow these preferences: outdoors and free first, no churches, pay only for unique sights. All prices are Nov 2026 unless stated.

**Weather:** southeast Sicily is the warmest, driest part of Italy in November (normals about 20/13–14 °C, 31–37% wet days; Palermo about 40%). Wind is the bigger risk on the north coast: Palermo saw gusts of 85+ km/h in mid-November 2026, and its airport is wind-prone.

**A loop that works:** cross from Calabria to Messina → Catania (base for Syracuse and Etna) → Taormina / Giardini Naxos → train along the north coast via Cefalù → Palermo → fly home from Palermo.

---

## Crossing from the mainland

- **By train down the Calabrian coast:** regional trains Naples → Paola → Villa San Giovanni (or Scilla / Reggio Calabria), about 6 h and €33 on regionals only. See [italy-guide.md](italy.md#calabria-naples--sicily-by-land) for the Calabria leg.
- **Villa San Giovanni → Messina:** the **Blu Jet** fast passenger ferry across the Strait, then a regional train from Messina to Catania, Taormina-Giardini or Palermo. Scilla → Villa San Giovanni → Blu Jet → Messina → Palermo was €17.15 in total (Nov 2026).
- **By overnight ferry:** Naples → Palermo with GNV or Tirrenia, about 20:00–22:30 → 07:00, from about €38–43 (Nov 2026). Saves a night and the long train. GNV fares are on its own departures calendar.
- **By air:** Ryanair Bari → Catania from about €15–17, Bari → Palermo €28–62 (Oct 2026, plus a cabin bag). There is no direct Naples → Catania flight (ITA routes via Rome).

## Catania

- **Sights:** the fish market (mornings), Piazza del Duomo, Via Crociferi.
- **Etna:** the AST bus to Rifugio Sapienza runs once a day; free trails around the Silvestri craters. Expect about 8/2 °C up there in November. Above 2,500 m only with a guide (about €160).
- **Syracuse** makes an easy day trip (below).
- **Where to stay:**
  - **The Yard Hostel** 9.1 Booking / 9.4 Hostelworld, kitchen with a stove. Its own Cloudbeds engine (`hotels.cloudbeds.com/en/reservation/EDMhxk`) was cheaper than Hostelworld (about €27–30/night there) in Nov 2026. Some reviews call it lively and loud late ("Partyhostel, eher laut").
  - **Eco Hostel** 8.1 (1,285), 400–450 m from the centre, single beds, stovetop, about €22–24/night (Hostelworld or direct); 14% of reviews below 7, basic shared bathrooms.
  - Ostello degli Elefanti: central, rooftop bar, 4-bed mixed dorms, about €33/night. Casa Verdi, about €30/night.

## Syracuse and Ortigia

**From Catania:** Trenitalia about 1 h (€8.80; e.g. 08:56 → 09:49, Nov 2026 timetable). Some Catania–Syracuse services are a rail-replacement bus the whole way (about 1–2 h): check the planner. **Interbus** coaches leave from the stops by Syracuse station and take about 1h20; **buy online**, because a full bus takes no standing passengers.

- **Optional:** the Papyrus Museum (€5), the one thing unique to Syracuse (the free version is the papyrus growing in Fonte Aretusa). Seafood dinner at L'Ancora da Giancarlo (12–15, 19:30–23).
- **Skippable:** the puppet museum (Palermo does it better), the Leonardo/Archimede museum, the catacombs, the churches.
- **Nearby:** Noto (baroque) + the **Vendicari nature reserve** coastal walk; Modica; Ragusa. LP's "Slice of Sicily" runs Catania → Syracuse → Noto → Ragusa → Agrigento.
- **Where to stay:** **LoL Hostel** 8.6 (1,039), next to the station, full kitchen and bed curtains, about €23/night. Only two hostels were open in Nov 2026; the other, Freedom, has no kitchen.

## Agrigento

Valley of the Temples, best at sunset. Syracuse → Agrigento by bus about 4 h with one change (about €19); Agrigento → Palermo about 2 h by train or bus. Villa Pirandello (private, 8.4, 450 m) about €39/night (Oct 2026). Skipping it (Syracuse → Palermo by train, about 4h55) saves only about 1h15.

## Taormina, Castelmola and Giardini Naxos

**Base:** Giardini Naxos is cheaper and flat. **B&B Villa Maria** (Vico Manganelli) is 600 m, 8 min on the flat, from Taormina-Giardini station, and about 10 min on foot from the *Villagonia Via Nazionale* bus stop.

**Buses (Interbus, Nov 2026 timetable):** IN09 Giardini → Taormina, then IN10 Taormina → Castelmola (about 50 min in total); IN10 also runs direct (e.g. 09:11), with later IN10 departures up at 10:11 and 11:11. Down from *Castelmola Traversa Tutti I Santi*: IN10 about every 1–2 h in the afternoon.

### Monte Veneretta hike + the walk down to the coast
- **Hike:** [Castelmola – Monte Veneretta](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/italy/sicily/castelmola-monte-veneretta), AllTrails 4.7 (58 reviews). 6.1 km out and back, 430 m of climbing, 2.5–3 h, **no shade**.
- **Lunch in Castelmola:** PierdeCat (4.9) or Bar Turrisi (almond wine).
- **Walk down (LP's Taormina walk):** Saraceni path → Porta Messina → Palazzo Corvaja → Piazza IX Aprile (cannolo from D'Amore) → Torre dell'Orologio → Palazzo Duca di Santo Stefano → Villa Comunale → Belvedere di Via Pirandello → **Mazzarò steps** (700+ steps, 45–60 min) → Via Nazionale → flat to Giardini.
  ▶ [Route in Google Maps (walking)](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/Bar+Turrisi,+Castelmola/Porta+Messina,+Taormina/Palazzo+Corvaja,+Taormina/Piazza+IX+Aprile,+Taormina/Torre+dell+Orologio,+Taormina/Palazzo+Duca+di+Santo+Stefano,+Taormina/Villa+Comunale+di+Taormina/Belvedere+di+Via+Pirandello,+Taormina/Sentiero+Taormina+Isola+Bella,+Taormina/B%26B+Villa+Maria,+Vico+Manganelli,+Giardini-Naxos/data=!4m2!4m1!3e2)
- **Seasonal closures:** the Mazzarò cable car was closed 9 Nov–8 Dec 2026 (hourly replacement shuttle); Isola Bella island was closed until mid-December 2026; the Teatro Antico closes at 16:00 in winter.

## Cefalù

**Trains (Nov 2026 timetable):** Taormina-Giardini → Cefalù about 3–4 h with a change at Messina (€15.10; the early connection had only a 6-min change, the next one a safe 1 h). Cefalù → Palermo Centrale about 55 min (€7.10). Buy in the Trenitalia app. It works as a stop on the way to Palermo with your bag stored.

- **Left luggage:** Bounce (at a travel agency 6 min from the station, €3.50, 09:00–21:00) or Radical Storage's Old Town office (€3.90).
- **La Rocca:** winter hours 08:00–17:00, **last entry 15:00** (visitcefalu.com). LP gives €5; the official site sells only a €10 combined pass. Up via Vicolo Saraceni, 1.5 km, 240 m of climbing; Tempio di Diana on the way.
- **Rocks walk:** from Porto Vecchio through the jetty arch → Bastione di Capo Marchiafava (sunset) → Capo Cefalù lighthouse.
  ▶ [Cefalù walk (walking)](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/Stazione+di+Cefal%C3%B9/Vicolo+Saraceni,+Cefal%C3%B9/Tempio+di+Diana,+Cefal%C3%B9/Spiaggia+del+Porto+Vecchio,+Cefal%C3%B9/Bastione+di+Capo+Marchiafava,+Cefal%C3%B9/Faro+di+Capo+Cefal%C3%B9/Stazione+di+Cefal%C3%B9/data=!4m2!4m1!3e2)
- **Eat (all open on Mondays):**
  - Arancina: **La Cefalù Nostrana** (4.9, from 09:00), Sfizi nostrani, Da Saro.
  - Lunch: **Pasta da qui!** (cheap fresh pasta, 11–17), **Mas Que Nada** (4.7, 12–16); also Doppio Zero (pizza), Passafiume, Galleria (seafood), Le Chat Noir, Tinchité.
  - Cannolo: **Cangelosi** (reopens 16:30), L'Angolo delle Dolcezze; gelato at Duomo Gelatieri dal 1952.
  - Sunset wine: **Le Petit Tonneau** (sea balcony) or Tentazioni Mediterranee. Kalapinta for craft beer (check hours).
  - Closed Mondays: Sfrigola, La Brace, Triscele, History Pinsa, Karma, Pasta e Pasti.
- *Church:* the Duomo.

## Palermo

### Outdoors

- **Monte Pellegrino** on foot via the Scala Vecchia.
- **Ustica** by hydrofoil (90 min): the Sentiero del Mezzogiorno coast walk to Punta Cavazzi and the Piscina Naturale. Winter hydrofoil times unchecked; wind cancels crossings.
- **San Vito lo Capo** further west (one hostel, Timbuktu).

### City
- **Ballarò and Capo markets** in the morning; La Cala; the No Mafia Memorial; the Vucciria at night.
- **Free walking tour:** *Palermo Past & Present* (GuruWalk 4.87 from 1,207 reviews; Freetour 9.7), daily 10:00, 2 h, tip-based, from outside the "Premier" shoe store, Via Maqueda 461.
- **Paid:** Streaty night food tour (Olivella and Vucciria, €64, 3 h, 4.94); Social Bike craft-beer ride (€55, 17:00, minimum 2) and antimafia ride (€42, runs solo).
- **Left out on purpose:** churches (Cappella Palatina, Monreale, the cathedrals and rooftops); Palazzo Alliata (tours Fri–Mon only); Le Terrazze del Sole (rooftop open March–October only); beach clubs and boat trips (closed in November).

### Getting to the airport
Train Palermo Centrale → Palermo Aeroporto (Punta Raisi) about 35–60 min, €6.80; the first trains leave around 06:00 (Nov 2026). Turkish Airlines flies Palermo → Istanbul with same-day connections to Seattle. Online check-in opens 24 h before. Check flight status on windy days.

## Transport tips

- **Trenitalia app** for regional tickets.
- **Interbus** for Taormina, Castelmola and the faster Syracuse–Catania coach; buy online when it might fill.
- Catania–Syracuse trains are often a replacement bus; compare with Interbus.
- **Flights home:** Air France award routings don't serve Palermo or Catania (Oct 2026); connect via Rome or Milan, or fly Turkish via Istanbul.
