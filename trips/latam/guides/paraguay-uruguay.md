**2. Paraguay + Uruguay (stops 7–11): Mon 21 Dec → Sat 2 Jan**

USD at the 24 Nov 2026 rates (open.er-api): UYU 40.6, PYG 6,350, ARS 1,576 per USD. Everything was live-checked on 24 Nov 2026 unless marked ≈.

**Day by day:**
- Mon 21: arrive Asunción, Costanera at sunset.
- Tue 22: Asunción old town, Loma San Jerónimo, Museo del Barro.
- Wed 23: 08:30 bus to Encarnación, Costanera.
- Thu 24: Trinidad + Jesús ruins; Nochebuena.
- Fri 25: beach and Costanera.
- Sat 26: EGA 13:30 overnight.
- Sun 27: Montevideo, Tristán Narvaja market, Rambla, candombe.
- Mon 28: 10:10 bus to Cabo Polonio.
- Tue 29: dunes to Valizas, sea lions.
- Wed 30: back to Montevideo.
- Thu 31: paper wars, Rambla fireworks.
- Fri 1 Jan: Colonia → ferry → night bus.
- Sat 2 Jan: Córdoba 06:00–07:10.

## Open issues: Paraguay + Uruguay

- **Book the EGA bus now.** Cama is sold out for Sat 26 Dec, and 17 semi-cama seats were left on 24 Nov. EGA runs only on Wed and Sat, so if this bus sells out the fallback is Posadas → Concordia → Salto → Montevideo, which arrives Sunday night and loses the Montevideo day. Buy in PYG on `reservadirecta.sittnet.net/Commerce/egapy` ($124, against $152 on the Uruguay shop). A foreign card should work there, but this is unverified.
- **1 Jan is the weak point.** There are no city or suburban buses (Día del Transportista), and COT's holiday timetable is unpublished. Ferries are confirmed on sale (Colonia Express at 08:30–20:15; Buquebus at 11:20, 17:01 and 22:40), and so is the Colonia Express bus + ferry through-ticket from Montevideo (12:45 → 17:30). Ask at Tres Cruces on 30 Dec. If there is no morning COT bus, take the through-ticket and drop the Colonia stopover, or move the whole day to Sat 2 Jan.
- **Colonia luggage storage is unconfirmed** (the terminal storage is reportedly closed). Pre-book a Radical Storage / Qeepl spot or travel light.
- **Rutas del Sol summer timetable:** only the winter 2026 one is online (10:10 → 14:30; return 15:10 → 20:10). Recheck mid-December, because summer adds buses.
- **Jesuit ruins on 25 Dec:** the hours are unconfirmed (special hours in past years), so plan them for 24 Dec. The PYG 40,000 foreigner fee comes from 2026 guides; SENATUR's page timed out.
- **Encarnación has no available hostel** (Frida and La Casa de Pedro are sold out on Agoda; there's nothing on HW). The pick, Departamentos De La Costa, has only 29 reviews. Terwindt (472 reviews) is the safer backup at +$51.
- **Cabo Polonio:** book early, because it's peak week (Viejo Lobo is already full on the 29th). Bring cash and groceries from Montevideo. The Green House kitchen/stove comes from Booking's facility list and review text, not photos.
- **Sierra de las Ánimas** doesn't fit with luggage, and is closed Mon–Fri. The only option is a Sun 27 Dec day trip, which needs a WhatsApp booking and a Spanish speaker. Recommended: skip.
- Prices at checkout: Plataforma10 and the ferry sites may add service or port fees, and Uruguay's VAT exemption for foreign cards may lower the hostel totals. Nothing was booked.
