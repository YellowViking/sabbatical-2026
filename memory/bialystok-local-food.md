# Białystok: one-night stop between Vilnius and Wrocław

*~4 zł ≈ $1. Prices and ratings checked Oct 2026.* A good overnight break on the rail route from Lithuania into Poland: compact centre, free palace gardens, Podlasie food (*kartacze*, potato babka, Tatar dishes) and a solid beer scene.

## Getting there and away (Oct 2026 timetable)

**From Vilnius:** LTG Link **12:35 → Mockava 14:52 (LT time) · Mockava 14:12 (PL time) → Białystok 17:07**, ~4½ h with one same-platform change at Mockava (20 min). **€23** non-refundable (flexible costs more). The only earlier train is 06:27 → 11:07 (€12.90). [LTG Link search](https://bilietas.ltglink.lt/journeys?oStop=17&dStop=246&fareClasses=BONUS_SCHEME_GROUP.ADULT%2C1).
- Clocks go back 1 h at the border.
- **Bring a passport:** Poland checks the Lithuanian border (announced until 30 Mar 2027).

**To Wrocław** (search on [Koleo](https://koleo.pl/), e.g. `koleo.pl/rozklad-pkp/bialystok/wroclaw-glowny/DD-MM-YYYY_08:00`):

| Train | Białystok → Wrocław Gł. | Changes | Fare |
|---|---|---|---|
| **IC 1626 "Leśmian"** | **10:15 → 16:43** | Direct | 85 zł |
| IC (morning) | 08:15 → 14:44 | Direct | |
| IC 145 "Wigry" → IC 1642 "Fredro" | 11:32 → Warszawa Centralna 13:07 · 13:20 → 17:27 | 1 (Warszawa, 13 min) | 86 zł |
| IC 1628 "Mickiewicz" | 12:15 → 19:12 | Direct | 85 zł |

Take the 11:32 or 12:15 if you want the free Wednesday morning at the Sybir Museum (opens 9:30).

**Station → centre:** the station is west of the centre. The main square is 1.6–1.8 km, a 25–30 min walk with a bag, or ~5 min by Bolt/Uber or city bus.

## Sights

Everything except the Sybir Museum is free and outdoors.

| Sight | Cost | When | From the main square |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Branicki Palace and gardens** (the "Versailles of Podlasie") | gardens free | evening walk or morning | 400 m |
| Kościuszko Market Square and Town Hall (Podlasie Museum) | square free | evening | 0 |
| Planty park and its lit fountain | free | evening | 550 m |
| Great Synagogue memorial (Jewish heritage) | free | anytime | 400 m |
| **Sybir Memorial Museum** (Siberian deportations; allow 1½–2 h) | **free on Wednesdays**, otherwise ~25 zł | Tue–Fri 9:30–17:00, closed Mon | ~2 km (taxi) |
| Ludwik Zamenhof Centre (Esperanto) | 8 zł | daytime | 650 m |

**Evening walk:** Branicki gardens, then the market square. **Białowieża Forest** (primeval forest, wild bison) is a day trip ~1 h 40 each way, worth a second night.
