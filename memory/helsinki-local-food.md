## Sample plan — **2n default**

| Day | Focus | Meals |
|---|---|---|
| **D0 AM land** | Drop bags · **Design District** walk · **Temppeliaukio** · waterfront · **Löyly** late | Market hall lunch · Kallio dinner |
| **D1** | **Suomenlinna** morning · optional Seurasaari/Lammassaari if energy · ferry → **Tallinn** | Picnic fortress · ferry snacks |
| **3n insert** | Full **Nuuksio** *or* **Porvoo** between D0 and exit | Supermarket trail lunch |

**Rain D1:** museums (Ateneum/Design) + Kallio cafés — skip open island wind.  
**1n emergency:** Suomenlinna half + one sauna *or* design only — then Tallinn.

## Food *(value lean)*

| Lean | Skip |
|---|---|
| **Market halls** lunch | Esplanadi tourist terraces only |
| **Kallio** casual / bars for dinner | Hotel restaurant default |
| Supermarket picnic Suomenlinna | Paying twice for mediocre harbour fish |
| Coffee + korvapuusti | Fine-dining FOMO on a 48h stop |

Finland is euro and pricey — same Nordic habits (tap water, cook if kitchen, limit bar rounds).

## Lodging (hostels & budget)

**Area:** **Kamppi / central** · **Kallio** OK for food/value.  
**Avoid:** far airport strip for a 2n city hit.

| Pick | Lean | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Citybox Helsinki** ⭐ | ~$135 ballpark | Social/value shortlist · near Kamppi |
| **Töölö Towers** | ~$95 | Quieter / further walk |
| **Scandic Helsinki Hub** | ~$214 | Most central splurge |

*Prefer free-cancel; ferry days are fixed once booked.* Mirrors itinerary §7.

## Money savers

| Tip | Detail |
|---|---|
| **HSL day vs singles** | Suomenlinna ferry day often loves a **day ticket** — math in app |
| **Sauna** | Löyly is a splurge with a point — skip a second paid museum if needed |
| **Groceries** | Lidl / S-market / K-Market for picnic |
| **Alcohol** | Pricey on-premise; Alko for takeaway if you care |
| **Tallinn next** | Food gets cheaper after HEL — don’t blow budget on two fancy HEL dinners |
| **Day rate** | Itinerary ~**$160**/n Citybox-class all-in |
