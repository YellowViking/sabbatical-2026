# Oslo → Stavanger by rail — Sørlandsbanen stopovers
*Public transport only, one traveller · researched Aug 2026 · [Oslo guide](oslo-local-food.md) · [Stavanger guide](stavanger-local-food.md) · [transit index](scandi-transit.md)*

The direct Go-Ahead **F5** (Sørlandsbanen) takes ~8h from Oslo S to Stavanger. Breaking it with one night turns a train day into a usable coastal-town afternoon for little extra fare.

## Best stop: Kristiansand

- A real stop on [Go-Ahead's Sørlandsbanen](https://go-aheadnordic.no/trafikk-ruter-linjer/sorlandsbanen), with direct F5 trains both from Oslo and on to Stavanger.
- Compact: the core sights are 7–12 min on foot from the station hotels, and [Voi scooters](https://www.voi.com/city/kristiansand) cover the longer Odderøya hop.
- **1 night** covers Kunstsilo, Posebyen and Baneheia on arrival, plus the waterfront; **0 nights** (straight through) keeps more slack for Stavanger weather.

The split costs only ~195 NOK (~$18) more than the through ticket; the real extra cost is the hotel night. Buy on [Entur](https://entur.no/) or [Go-Ahead](https://go-aheadnordic.no/planner).

### A Kristiansand afternoon
- From the 11:51 arrival: drop bags (Citybox stores luggage before its 15:00 check-in) → **Kunstsilo** for ~2h (Tuesday hours were 11:00–17:00 in 2026) → **Posebyen** (old wooden quarter) → **Baneheia** woods → **Fiskebrygga** for dinner.
- Outdoor version: skip Kunstsilo and spend longer in Baneheia / Ravnedalen, then Bystranda or Odderøya.
- It is one good afternoon and evening, not a hidden two-night city.

## Other stops considered (and why not)

| Stop | Verdict |
|---|---|
| **Arendal** | Attractive and Voi-served, but on the R50 branch from Nelaug: two extra changes and ~1h26 more than Kristiansand. Better for a dedicated coast trip. |
| **Egersund** | Direct main line and compact, ~1h from Stavanger (L5 local trains too). The faience museum is closed on Tuesdays. Close enough to Stavanger that an overnight adds little. |
| **Kongsberg** | Direct main line and walkable, but the central Mining Museum closed for renovation in Aug 2026, and the Silver Mines are 8 km out with limited days. |
| **Bø / Lunde / small main-line stations** | Real stops, but the nature/canal sights need local transport and lodging is thin. |
| **Grimstad / Risør / Lillesand / Mandal / Flekkefjord** | Worthwhile southern towns but off the rail line; all need a bus detour. |
