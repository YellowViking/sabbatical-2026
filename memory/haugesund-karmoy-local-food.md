## Getting around

Use **Entur** for the whole journey and **Kolumbus** for local tickets. Recheck the night before because Sunday service is sparse.

## Sample 2n plan

### Arrival

- Walk **Smedasundet**, Haraldsgata and the harbour; add Haraldshaugen if daylight and energy remain.
- If staying at Home Amanda, use the included evening meal rather than forcing another restaurant decision.

### Full weekday — Karmøy spine

| Leg | Verified example |
|---|---|
| Haugesund → Avaldsnes | **08:25–08:49 · 210** |
| Avaldsnes → Åkrasanden | **11:49–12:29 · 210** |
| Åkrasanden → Skudeneshavn | **13:29–13:46 · 210** |
| Skudeneshavn → Haugesund | **16:54–18:17 · 210** |

- **Avaldsnes:** Nordvegen History Centre + St. Olav's Church + Bukkøy cultural-landscape walk. The staffed Viking Farm is closed after its summer season.
- **Åkrasanden:** this schedule permits only a short beach stop. In good weather, drop Avaldsnes or shorten Skudeneshavn to make room for the coastal walk.
- **Skudeneshavn:** old white wooden town and harbour; the streetscape works even when seasonal museums are shut.
- In hard rain, keep Nordvegen and Skudeneshavn; cut the exposed beach.

## Food / money

- Home Amanda's meal package is the simplest cost-control option.
- With Banken or Bris, use the kitchen / supermarket for one meal and spend selectively on local herring or seafood.
- A Kolumbus day product may beat separate fares for the multi-stop Karmøy circuit; compare in-app before boarding.
- Utsira is a real third-day option, not filler: roughly **70 minutes each way by scheduled boat**, but verify a same-day return before adding the night.
