# Brno: a one-night stop on the Prague–Graz line

*Checked Oct 2026.* Cheap, student-heavy, and right on the direct Prague–Vienna–Graz railjet line, so it breaks the long run south for little extra cost. **Most indoor sights close on Mondays**: arrive on a Monday for an outdoor/viewpoint afternoon and do the underground sights on Tuesday morning.

## Getting there and away (Oct 2026 timetable)

| Leg | Train | Times | Cheapest seller |
|---|---|---|---|
| From Prague | ČD rj 277 "Metropolitan" | Praha hl.n. 09:37 → Brno hl.n. 12:13, direct (2 h 36) | **379 CZK** on cd.cz · RegioJet 11:01 → 13:40 €9.70–11.80 |
| **To Graz** | rj 53 "Vindobona" | Brno **09:18 → Graz Hbf 13:19**, direct (4 h) | **ÖBB €25** saver (non-refundable); ČD charges 996 CZK for the same train |
| To Graz, later | RJ 251 | Brno 13:18 → Graz 17:19, direct | ÖBB €25.70 |

Take the 13:18 if you want the Labyrinth and Ossuary on the morning you leave.

**Bags:** "Luggage Storage 24/7" lockers inside Brno hl.n., from about €0.70/h (Google, 4.3★).

**Half-day + morning plan:**
- **Afternoon:** Zelný trh → Old Town Hall tower → Freedom Square terrace → walk up to Špilberk → sunset at Denisovy sady.
- **Evening:** the student food and beer scene (open on Mondays).
- **Next morning (if leaving on the 13:18):** Labyrinth at 09:00 → Ossuary.
