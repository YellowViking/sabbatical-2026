- It is an actual stop on [Go-Ahead's official Sørlandsbanen](https://go-aheadnordic.no/trafikk-ruter-linjer/sorlandsbanen), with direct F5 trains from Oslo and onward to Stavanger.
- No duration is selected. **1n** can cover Posebyen + Baneheia on arrival and Kunstsilo / Odderøya before the next train; **0n** keeps more weather slack for Stavanger.
- The center passes the mobility rule: most core places are 7–12 minutes from the bed, and [Voi is active](https://www.voi.com/city/kristiansand) for the longer Odderøya hop.
- Population was not used as a quality proxy. Kristiansand wins on direct rail, exact-date lodging, walkable nature/culture and weather resilience.
