*Good length **3 nights** (4 to hold a spare weather day) · nature-first · prices seen Aug–Sep 2026 at ~10.5–11 NOK ≈ $1*
*Other Scandinavian cities and the transit spine: [scandi-transit.md](scandinavia-transit.md) · arriving by rail from Oslo: [stopovers](oslo-stavanger-stopovers.md)*

### Preikestolen shuttle — how it works
1. Buy the **return ticket for the exact outbound date and time** at pulpitrock.no and choose **Stavanger byterminal (stop 2)**. The outbound is valid only on that departure.
2. Walk to **Stavanger bussterminal on Jernbaneveien**, beside the railway station on the south side of Breiavatnet.
3. Follow the numbered **spor/stop** signs to **2** (a boarding bay, not bus route 2). Arrive 20 min early and look for the Pulpit Rock Tours / Boreal coach.
4. Show the ticket from the email. The ride to the Preikestolen basecamp is ~50 min; the operator estimates 3–5 hours for the hike.
5. The return half works on **any scheduled return within four days**, but seats are **first come, first served** — queue early for the bus you want.

Example (Sep 2026, weekday): 08:00 from stop 2 → basecamp 08:50. Returns from the basecamp at 10:00, 12:35, 13:15, 14:00, 15:30, 16:15, 17:45, 18:15, 19:15 and 20:30 (last). From an 08:50 arrival, 13:15 gives a ~4h25 trail window; 14:00 if slower. Customer service +47 51 59 90 60 (Mon–Fri 08:00–16:00).

### Hike-day rules
- **Weather gate at ~06:15:** check the summit webcam, Yr radar, wind and official warnings. Use the Yr forecast for the **Preikestolen summit itself** (Yr location 1-15763), not Stavanger city. Go if the summit isn't in cloud and there's no warning; skip in zero visibility, high wind or an incoming rain band.
- Start early to beat the tour buses (~09:00) and the afternoon rain that often arrives around noon.
- Pack layers, waterproof shell, water, lunch, grippy shoes. Don't linger on the exposed plateau when rain comes.
- If the gate fails: Sverd i fjell in a dry gap, then a museum; take the Lysefjord cruise for cliff views without the hike.

### Stavanger parkrun (Saturdays)
- 09:30 start at **Gamlingen, Mosvangen 18**, by Mosvatnet. 5 km: clockwise around Mosvatnet plus an out-and-back climb to Vålandstårnet; puddles and muddy trail sections in rain.
- Bring your scannable parkrun barcode (no barcode, no result). Coffee with the group at the Gamlingen café afterwards.
- Buses X60/E90 stop at **Mosvatnet Camping** beside the start; routes 6/7 stop across the lake.
- Gamlingen pool + sauna next door: 100 NOK adult, 1.5h, bathing cap required (2026); check the municipal page for closures.

Stavanger is oil-money expensive. Win with boat shrimp, fish-market soup, smoked fish and Pedersgata, not Skagenkaien tourist terraces. **Mathallen Stavanger has closed.**

## Where to stay

| Area | Why |
|---|---|
| **Sentrum / Vågen** | Walk to the shuttle, food and Gamle Stavanger |
| **Storhaug** (east of the centre) | Quiet residential streets near the Pedersgata food strip, 10–12 min to the bus terminal |

**Avoid as a hike base:** Forus / Sandnes / Hinna — cheap hotels, but a daily transit slog and you risk missing the early shuttle. There is **no curtain-pod hostel** in the centre (Pop-Up has closed); Central Stavanger Guesthouse at Brødregata 23 is a dorm hostel with shared bathrooms.

**Lysefjordgata 12 details:** the 16 m² queen with a quiet street view beat the 14 m² inner-courtyard room (full bed, stairs only, 97 NOK more) — no review shows the courtyard room is quieter. Both rates had a cancellation charge from the start (~45% of the total) and include a 300 NOK cleaning fee. Caveats: steep uphill approach with luggage, stairs to the kitchen, an outside entry stair without a handrail, functional rooms; one report of thin walls. Booking's room table says "private kitchen", but guests describe shared kitchens — don't count on a kitchenette.

**Channel check (Aug 2026, same rooms):** Booking was cheapest and had the best refund terms. The operator's direct engine (SimplyCity / Smoobu) matched the price with a worse (no-refund) policy; Agoda was ~383 NOK more; Expedia / Hotels.com and Trip.com ~$25 more. Google's 4.7/11 rating merges the operator's two addresses, so use the address-specific OTA reviews. A Cloudbeds page under a similar name is the Brødregata dorm hostel, not this property.
