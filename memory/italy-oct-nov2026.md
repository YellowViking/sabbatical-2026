# Italy (+ Sicily): Tue 20 Oct → ~Sat 21 Nov 2026 (draft, about 32 nights)

*Built on 10–11 Oct from Lonely Planet *Italy*, Open-Meteo 10-year normals (2016–2025), live Booking.com availability and the event websites. **Draft:** Italian train times and the Matera links aren't checked yet. Nothing is booked.*

## Why this order

- **Weather decides it.**
  - Italy cools about 1 °C a week, and the south is 4–5 °C warmer than the north. Going north → south keeps you at about 17–20 °C the whole time.
  - **Naples/Amalfi is the wettest region in November** (about 50–60% wet days), so it stays short and swappable.
  - **Southeast Sicily is the warmest and driest** (about 20 °C, 31–37% wet days in mid/late November), so it gets the last and longest block.
- **The flight home is at the end of Schengen** (day 90 = 21 Nov).
  - No Italian airport has a nonstop to Seattle in November (Google Flights, 19–21 Nov), so every option has one connection.
  - **Catania works as well as Rome:** Turkish via Istanbul, about $645 cash on 21 Nov. Plan to pay with points.
