# Bergen + fjords: food, nature, sights, lodging & transport
*Bergen city plus the Hardanger / Sognefjord pocket · nature-first · ~10.5–11 NOK ≈ $1 (Aug 2026) · **pack rain gear***
*All-legs transport index: [scandi-transit.md](scandi-transit.md) · Country money rules: [scandi-money-tips.md](scandi-money-tips.md)*

## How long, what shape

| | |
|---|---|
| **Nights** | **6 is a good default** · **4** = tight city + fjord · **7** = kayak / Rallarvegen / weather buffer |
| **Shape** | **2–3n Bergen + 2–3n Aurland / Flåm / Undredal**, not six city nights |
| **Pace** | One clear mountain day in the city · one hard fjord hike · **fog = no blind cruise** |
| **Season** | Late Aug / Sep: rain is normal. Shell + pack cover mandatory. Book Flåmsbana and the matching cruise early |

| Nights | Do |
|---|---|
| **4** | 2n Bergen (Bryggen + one peak) + 2n fjord (Flåmsbana + cruise *or* Rimstigen) |
| **6** | 3n city + 3n fjord base (plan below) |
| **7** | + Nærøyfjord kayak *or* Rallarvegen *or* a pure weather buffer |

| Idea | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Bryggen walk | **Yes** |
| **One** of Fløyen **or** Ulriken (not two cable-car days) | Yes |
| Stoltzekleiven → Ulriken ridge | Yes if clear and legs are good |
| KODE | Rain day |
| **Flåmsbana** | **Yes**, book early |
| Nærøyfjord cruise | Yes on a clear day or afternoon burn-off |
| Stegastein | Yes if time and weather allow |
| Sleep in Undredal / Aurland rather than Flåm | Quieter nights |
| Rimstigen (Gudvangen/Bakka) | Yes on dry rock only |
| Torget tourist fish tents | **Skip** sit-down meals |
| Trolltunga | Usually no (see below) |

## Sample plans

### 6 nights (3 Bergen + 3 fjord)

| Day | Base | Nature / sights | Food |
|---|---|---|---|
| D0 | Bergen | Arrive · **Bryggen** alleys · harbour edge | **Trekroneren** pølse · **Godt Brød** skillingsbolle |
| D1 | Bergen | Clear: **Stoltzekleiven → ridge → Ulriken** (cable down optional) *or* hike up Fløyen | Packed lunch · **Pingvinen** fiskesuppe / plukkfisk |
| D2 | Bergen | Rain: **KODE** · easy Fløyen if it breaks · Sandviken stroll | Picnic / kitchen |
| D3 | → fjord | **Bergensbanen Bergen→Myrdal → Flåmsbana → Flåm** · bus/taxi to Undredal or Aurland | Train picnic · simple dinner |
| D4 | fjord | **Rimstigen** if dry · else valley walk / goat cheese · evening **Ægir** in Flåm | Picnic + Ægir |
| D5 | fjord | **Nærøyfjord** cruise (push if morning fog) · **Stegastein** shuttle | **Marianne Bakeri** (Aurland) |
| D6 | exit | Flåmsbana up to Myrdal → east *or* back to BGO for a flight | Bakery + snacks |

**4n:** drop the KODE day and Stegastein, 2+2. **7n:** add an empty weather day or a kayak.

### Rainy-week Bergen (city only, no headline sights)
Useful when the fjord loop is saved for later or the forecast is a washout.

| Idea | Notes |
|---|---|
| **KODE** art museums | **Closed Mondays.** One ~200 NOK ticket covered all city KODE buildings the same day (Sep 2026). Start with **Rasmus Meyer (Munch)**, then Permanenten / Lysverket |
| **Troldhaugen** (Grieg museum, garden, café) | Tue–Sun 10:00–16:00; the villa itself was under renovation in 2026. Light rail/bus, not a long exposed walk |
| **Nordnes peninsula** + aquarium / harbour sauna | No-regret wet-day fallback |
| **Sandviken wooden lanes → Gamle Bergen grounds → Sandviken sjøbad** | Best use of a clear day if you are saving Fløyen/Ulriken |
| **Løvstakken** | Only when the trail has dried; turn round in cloud |
| Norwegian Fisheries Museum | Rain filler near Sandviken |
| **Kulturnatt i Bergen** (Culture Night) | One Friday in September, 18:00–00:00: free/late museums, galleries, concerts across sentrum (2026: Fri 11 Sep) |
| **Voss day trip** | Direct train ~1h15 each way: Vangsvatnet lakeside, **Bordalsgjelet** gorge (free, from town), **Hangurstoppen** (4 km, 450 m). Outside Skyss Zone A |
| Osterøy / Kossdalsvingane | Dry-day alternative; check Skyss connections first |

Don't force Fedje, Solund or Balestrand into a wet front just to fill a calendar.

## Getting around

### Apps & tickets
| Where | App / site |
|---|---|
| Bergen city | **Skyss** app (journey planner + tickets) |
| Norway planner | **Entur** |
| Trains | **Vy** (Bergen S ↔ Voss / Myrdal / Oslo) |
| Flåm railway + combos | **flamsbana.no** / Norway's Best |
| Stegastein | Operator shuttle (book) |
| Fjord buses | Check Entur the night before |

**Skyss Zone A adult fares (from 1 May 2026):**

| Product | Fare | Use |
|---|---:|---|
| Single · 60 min | 51 NOK | ≤6 rides in a week |
| 24 hours | 136 NOK | One transit-heavy day |
| **7 days** | **315 NOK** | Breaks even at 7 singles; best for a hillside base (e.g. Montana) |

Buy in the Skyss app with a bank card and start it just before boarding. No tap on/off with an app ticket; show it at inspection. **Zone A covers** city buses, Bergen Light Rail (incl. to the airport), Fantoft/Troldhaugen, Askøy/Nordhordland local boats and local trains to Arna/Trengereid. **Not covered:** FlixBus, Flybussen, the Voss train beyond Trengereid, separately priced express boats.

**Fallback:** on a bus, buy a single or 24h ticket from the driver by card or cash (paper slip = ticket). For light rail, buy before boarding (app, machine, webshop or SMS; SMS often fails on foreign numbers).

### By car: Hardanger + Sogn + the scenic routes
A rental car opens a loop that the rail/boat version can't. Rough Bergen rental: ~US$194–220 for 4 days before tolls, ferries and charging (Sep 2026).

| Area | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Norheimsund** (~1h15 from Bergen) | **Steinsdalsfossen**, walk-behind waterfall right by the main road, free. Hardangerfjord kayaks ~$55–85 pp (2026) |
| **Hardangerfjord driving loop** (~135 km from Norheimsund) | Øystese (Kunsthuset Kabuso) → Kvanndal → car ferry to **Utne** (orchards, folk museum) → Jondal → Rosendal |
| **Eidfjord** | **Vøringsfossen**; Hardangervidda Natursenter for a rain hour. **Kjeåsen farm**, 600 m above the fjord: the narrow road is one-way, **up on the hour, down on the half-hour**. Pointless in full cloud |
| **Odda** | **Buarbreen** glacier hike (lower Buarbreen) or the easier **Bondhusvatnet** glacier lake. Trolltunga trailhead (Skjeggedal) is 13 km away, but the trail is 23 km / ~10h / ~1,000 m and the season usually ends in **early September** |
| **Aurland / Flåm / Lærdal** | Stegastein, the **Aurlandsfjellet "Snow Road"** over to **Lærdalsøyri** (old wooden town) |
| **Sognefjellet → Geiranger** | High mountain road; Geiranger viewpoints and Storsæterfossen. The Geiranger–Hellesylt car ferry cost NOK 1,680 for 65 min (Sep 2026), so drive round instead |
| **Gaularfjellet** | 114 km national tourist route between Balestrand and the coast: **Utsikten** viewpoint, **Likholefossen / Fossestien**. Combine with Vikafjellet via the Dragsvik–Vangsnes ferry (route then runs through Voss) |

Check road status on Vegvesen and the forecast for each overnight town, not Bergen's.

**Montana booking notes (2026):**
- Book **direct**: in Sep 2026 the official site beat the cheapest comparable OTA basket (Agoda) by ~12% without membership. Agoda's teaser rates excluded VAT; Booking and Trip.com were dearer still.
- **HI membership pays for itself on one stay.** Member promo code `HIMEMBER2026` stacked with the direct sale (~10% off; a valid HI card is checked at arrival). Buy the card for your country of residence: HI USA US$20/yr, or £15 through the [global HI portal](https://member.hihostels.com/).
- Room inventory is fragmented: **one-night searches surface private rooms that multi-night searches hide**, and the cart refuses the same room category on nonconsecutive dates, so a chain of room types needs separate linked reservations. Ask reception to link them and hold bags between the 10:00 checkout and 15:00 check-in.
- Montana's cancellation cutoffs were 3–4 days before arrival on flexible rates.
