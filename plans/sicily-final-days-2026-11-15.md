## Sun 15: Monte Veneretta + Taormina walk
- **Getting up there:** from Villa Maria, walk about 10 min to the *Villagonia Via Nazionale* stop by the station. Take Interbus **IN09 at 09:15** to Taormina, then **IN10** to Castelmola (arrives 10:05). Alternatively, IN10 direct at 09:11. Later IN10 buses up: 10:11, 11:11.
- **Hike:** [Castelmola – Monte Veneretta](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/italy/sicily/castelmola-monte-veneretta). AllTrails 4.7 (58 reviews), 6.1 km out and back, 430 m of climbing, 2.5–3 h, no shade.
- **Lunch:** PierdeCat (4.9) or Bar Turrisi (almond wine) in Castelmola.
- **Walking down (LP):** Saraceni path → Porta Messina → Palazzo Corvaja → Piazza IX Aprile (cannolo from D'Amore) → Torre dell'Orologio → Palazzo Duca di Santo Stefano → Villa Comunale → Belvedere di Via Pirandello → **Mazzarò steps** (700+ steps, 45–60 min) → Via Nazionale → flat to Villa Maria.
  ▶ [Route in Google Maps (walking)](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/Bar+Turrisi,+Castelmola/Porta+Messina,+Taormina/Palazzo+Corvaja,+Taormina/Piazza+IX+Aprile,+Taormina/Torre+dell+Orologio,+Taormina/Palazzo+Duca+di+Santo+Stefano,+Taormina/Villa+Comunale+di+Taormina/Belvedere+di+Via+Pirandello,+Taormina/Sentiero+Taormina+Isola+Bella,+Taormina/B%26B+Villa+Maria,+Vico+Manganelli,+Giardini-Naxos/data=!4m2!4m1!3e2)
- **Bus down instead of walking:** IN10 from *Castelmola Traversa Tutti I Santi* at 13:09, 15:09 or 16:09.
- **Closed right now:** the Mazzarò cable car (9 Nov–8 Dec, replacement shuttle hourly) and Isola Bella island (until about 15 Dec). Teatro Antico closes at 16:00 in winter.
