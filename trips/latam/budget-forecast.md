# Latin America (route v5): budget forecast, 7 Dec 2026 → 4 Mar 2027

Built 24 Nov 2026 for the [v5 itinerary](itinerary.md) (86 days, 10 countries), in the same format as the [Europe forecast](../europe-2026/budget-forecast.md) and following its lessons:
- Beds are budgeted at the **dorm picks actually checked** for the exact dates, with private rooms as a separate buffer.
- Transport is priced **per leg**, with a reserve for route changes.
- The award flights are valued explicitly.

All prices are in **USD**. The v4 Brazil-only forecast is in git history.

## (c) Transport, tours and fees

**Transport, ≈ $1.25–1.60k:**
- The biggest items:
  - Arequipa → Lima → Panama City, $326.
  - EGA Encarnación → Montevideo, $124.
  - Purmamarca → San Pedro, $73–88.
  - Air Panama to David, $67.
  - Curitiba → Foz, $49.
  - Colonia → Buenos Aires ferry, $48.
  - Buenos Aires → Córdoba, $44–48.
  - Sucre → La Paz, $56.
- Chirripó descent day (Thu 11 Feb): no San Gerardo bus fits, so a taxi to San Isidro ≈ $34–45 (added 26 Nov).
- **Plan B** (Florianópolis instead of Paraguay, 21–27 Dec): ≈ $255 in beds and buses against ≈ $256 for Plan A, so the totals don't change ([guide](guides/florianopolis.md)).
- Border fees: Costa Rica exit $8, Nicaragua entry ≈ $12–14 (the $10 tourist stamp plus ≈ $1–4 border charges, cash).
- Leg by leg: [route-v5-brainstorm §12 and §15](route-v5-brainstorm.md); each guide's "Getting here".

**Core sights and park fees, ≈ $0.32k:**
- Brazil and Paraguay: Iguaçu, Brazil side ≈ $22 and Argentine side ≈ $38; Jesuit ruins ≈ $6.
- Chile, Bolivia and Peru: Valle de la Luna and San Pedro entries ≈ $15–25; Isla del Sol fees ≈ $5; Colca boleto S/70 ≈ $19.
- Panama: Volcán Barú park $5–7.
- Costa Rica:
  - Chirripó SINAC admission 2 days ≈ $41 with VAT.
  - Marino Ballena ≈ $6–12; Santa Elena reserve ≈ $18.
  - Arenal or Río Celeste ≈ $15; Rincón de la Vieja ≈ $15.
- Nicaragua: Ometepe ferries and guides ≈ $30–40.
- Free: Condorito, Quebrada de las Conchas by bike and bus, Garganta del Diablo, Hornocal (small community fee), El Chollín hot river, and most city walks.

**Admin, ≈ $0.29–0.43k:**
- **Brazil e-visa, US$80.90**: required for US citizens since 10 Apr 2025, up to 10 business days, apply now. ✅ The official portal is [brazil.vfsevisa.com](https://brazil.vfsevisa.com/); the fee is ≈ from a summary of US State Department pages.
- Travel insurance ≈ $150–250 for 3 months.
- eSIMs ≈ $60–100.
- Bolivia no longer needs a visa for US citizens (since Dec 2025, ≈).
- The yellow-fever vaccine, if you don't have it, costs ≈ $200–350 in the US. Not included above.

## (d) Levers

- **Down:**
  - A third drink a day costs +$0.25k; a dry week saves about $0.05k.
  - Self-cater more in Uruguay, Chile and Costa Rica, where hostel kitchens are confirmed at the picks. That's worth about $5–8/day there.
  - Encarnación: if a hostel bed frees up, it saves about $40 on the flat.
  - Take the Boquete bus instead of the Air Panama flight: about −$45, but Carnaval Friday buses are packed.
  - **Arequipa → Panama, three open options** ([Panama City guide](guides/panama-city.md#getting-here-three-options-arequipa--panama-city)):
    - A, cash same day: ≈ $345 including the 2nd Arequipa night.
    - B, Lima night + Copa on United miles: ≈ $90 + 17,500.
    - C, overland via [Huacachina + Paracas](guides/huacachina-paracas.md) + the award: ≈ $185 + 17,500, and +3 days (≈ +$125 living).
- **Up:**
  - Private rooms everywhere: about +$1.5–2k.
  - The 5 spare days left: +$0.21k.
  - Bolivian blockades: extra nights or a flight, +$0.1–0.3k (the route-change reserve covers one event).

## (e) Against the backpacker consensus

Recent reports put Central and South America at **$50–70/day** in hostels (r/backpacking, Jul 2026) and Central America at **$25–60/day** (2026). Living here comes to ≈ $42.5/day before transport. With intercity transport, tours, fees and admin, it's ≈ $75–80/day: above the pure-backpacker figure, because this route covers 10 countries in 12 weeks and has three flights. The pace warning from [route-v5-brainstorm §14](route-v5-brainstorm.md) applies.
