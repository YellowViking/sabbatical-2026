# Europe 2026: pre-trip budget forecast

This is the cost model built **before and during** the trip, for an 83-night baseline route. The trip as travelled was 89 nights and went differently in places: Gotland and Croatia's coast were skipped, more of Italy was added, and Sicily was added. See the [trip log](README.md). Actual spending was tracked privately from card statements and isn't published. Keep this page as the **template for budgeting the next trip** (see [Latin America](../latam/README.md)).

## Bottom line

**Carry about $17k; expected cash spend about $15.6–16.3k** for the 83-night fall route.

- **Living:** about **$11.8k**.
  - Lodging ~$6.5k
  - Food ~$2.7k
  - Three drinks a day ~$1.8k
  - Local and routine costs ~$0.85k
- **On top of living:** a 10% cushion (~$1.2k), all transport (~$1.6–1.8k), major sights (~$0.75–0.9k), and insurance plus an eSIM (~$0.3–0.6k).

## Assumptions

- **Rooms:**
  - Private rooms first in the Baltics, Slovenia and Croatia.
  - Elsewhere, take a private room when the final rate is **≤ $110 a night or ≤ $40 above** a good dorm bed.
  - Keep dorms when a private room costs more than 2× a dorm bed, is well above that cap, or isn't available.
- **Food:** groceries and kitchen cooking plus good-value local meals, excluding alcohol.
- **Drinks:** 3 standard drinks a day (beer, a glass of wine or similar), 249 in total, at a country-weighted mix of shop, happy-hour and local-bar prices. That averages about **$7.10 a drink**. Three cocktails or premium craft pours a day would need more.

**Ways to cut it:**
- Skipping Gotland and adding 2 Riga nights saves about $250–300, including the ferry.
- A home flight on points saves about $0.4–0.6k in cash.
- Cutting a week of Italy saves about $1.2k of living costs.
- More nights in cheap bases (Riga) and hotel points on expensive cities (Stockholm, Italy) also help.

## Points vs cash (how the award flights worked out)

| Flight | Paid | Cash fare at the time | Value |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Seattle → Copenhagen**, SAS A350, nonstop, 23 kg bag included | 25,500 Virgin points + $5.60 (via Amex/Chase transfers) | Best cash option: $213 on Icelandair (slower, with a stop) | Arrived 35 h earlier, 4 h shorter, nonstop, bag included. Delta wanted 89.9k for the same seat |
| **Bergen → Stockholm**, Finnair, nonstop | 3,250 Avios + $42.70 (Amex points at a +30% transfer bonus) | $232 | **7.6¢ per Amex point**, five times the 1.3–1.5¢ bar |

**Rule used:** redeem only above about 1.3–1.5¢ a point, transfer points only at the moment of booking, and keep points for long-haul or premium cabins. Short-haul cash fares are usually cheap. See [flights: points vs cash](../../research/flights-points-vs-cash.md).

**Sights budget:** keep about $750–900 for the major paid sights and access, such as Tivoli, the Munch Museum or the National Museum, the Preikestolen shuttle, Plitvice, Dubrovnik and Italy's museums. Choosing free nature first keeps this bounded.
